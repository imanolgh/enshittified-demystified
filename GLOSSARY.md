# Ownership

How the project describes who owns a brand and how private equity sits above it.

## Language

**Ownership chain**:
The ordered list of owners from a brand (or, later, a location) up through each owner above it, ending at the first private equity owner or at the top owner, e.g. Dunkin' → Inspire Brands → Roark Capital, or for a location, Bright Smiles Dental → Smile Partners → Fund X. A brand can have more than one chain.
_Avoid_: Ownership tree, path (in user-facing text)

**Hop**:
One link between two neighbours in an ownership chain: an owner above what it owns, or the operator above a location.
_Avoid_: Step, level, link (when counting)

**Separation degree**:
The number of hops from a brand (or, later, a location) to one specific private equity owner, where a fund and the investment firm that manages it count as one owner. A fund or firm that owns the brand directly is at separation degree 1.
_Avoid_: Distance, depth

**PE-owned**:
The category of a brand, company or location that has at least one ownership chain to a private equity fund or firm with no minority hop, whether the fund or firm owns it directly or through intermediate companies. A public company counts when a private equity owner holds more than half of its votes.
_Avoid_: PE-backed, PE-affiliated

**PE minority stake**:
The category of a brand, company or location whose every ownership chain to private equity contains at least one minority hop. A fund manager's ordinary passive holding in a public company is not one.
_Avoid_: Partial PE, PE-invested

**Minority hop**:
A hop where a cited document states a stake under 50% or calls it a minority investment. A hop with no stated stake counts as control.
_Avoid_: Partial stake

**Co-owner**:
One of two or more owners whose hops to the same brand or company overlap, such as THL and Linden at Smile Doctors. An owner who joins does not replace the others unless a source says one of them sold or exited. Co-owners are not sources disagreeing.
_Avoid_: Partner, joint owner (in user-facing text)

**Pending deal**:
An announced acquisition that has not completed, such as Nestlé's agreed sale of Nature's Bounty to Yellow Wood Partners. It is not a hop and does not change a category until it completes. When the announcement gives an expected closing, such as "by the first half of 2027", the deal carries that range. A deal that is called off, or has not completed within 18 months, is no longer pending.
_Avoid_: Proposed acquisition, rumored deal

**Formerly PE-owned**:
History shown for a brand, company or location whose chain to a private equity fund or firm has ended, with the years it lasted. It is history, not a current category. A chain that has only gone unconfirmed has not ended; it shows its last confirmed date instead.
_Avoid_: Ex-PE, previously backed

**No PE evidence found**:
The category of a brand, company or location that was checked against every source the product requires for it and has no chain to private equity, always shown with the date it was checked.
_Avoid_: Independent, PE-free, not PE-owned

## Entities

**Brand**:
A name a company sells under at several locations or on products, such as Dunkin' or Ray-Ban. A brand owns nothing. Its owner is usually a company, but a source may name a fund, investment firm or family directly, and a brand can pass from one owner to another.
_Avoid_: Label, marque, chain (as its own kind of thing)

**Company**:
A legal entity, such as a corporation, LLC, partnership, trust, nonprofit or government body, that can own, be owned or run locations. A franchisee, a rollup and a public company are all companies.
_Avoid_: Business (as the thing that owns), organization, firm (except investment firm)

**Location**:
One place where one company at a time serves customers under zero or more brands, such as a store, gym or dental office. A new operator keeps the same location; a move to a new address starts a new one.
_Avoid_: Store, site, branch, outlet (as the general term)

**Family**:
One person or a family who owns a company in their own name, named only as sources name them. It is an owner, never a profile of a person.
_Avoid_: Founder, individual, person (as an owner)

**Industry**:
What line of business an entity is in, such as dental care, restaurants or gyms. It is not the entity's ownership category.
_Avoid_: Category, vertical, sector

**Operator**:
The company that runs a location. A franchisee is the operator of its stores. A location is company-run when its operator is in the brand owner's ownership chain, and franchised when it isn't.
_Avoid_: Manager, franchisee (as the general term)

## Owners

**Owner kind**:
What sort of owner an entity is: private equity, venture capital, real estate, other investment fund (hedge, credit and other pooled funds), conglomerate, family, employee-owned, trust, public company, nonprofit, government, or cooperative. It is a fact about the owner, not about any single hop. A fund and the firm that manages it can have different kinds, as when a private equity firm runs a real estate fund.
_Avoid_: Ownership type, owner category

**Certified Independent**:
A future badge a brand, company or location earns when automated checks of official records show no PE ownership. The only way the product will call anything independent.
_Avoid_: Verified independent, independent (unqualified)

**Owner type not confirmed**:
What the product shows when an owner's owner kind cannot be established from any source, or its sources disagree. The ownership chain is still shown.
_Avoid_: Unknown owner, unclassified

**Investment firm**:
A company that manages funds, as the public knows it, such as KKR or L Catterton, even when it is registered as several advisers. Its owner kind says whether it is a PE firm. A public company that owns an investment firm, such as Blackstone Inc. or The Goldman Sachs Group, is a separate company above it.
_Avoid_: Sponsor, GP, asset manager (as entity labels)

**Fund**:
A pool of money that one investment firm manages at a time, and that owns or lends to companies. Most of its money comes from outside investors, so the firm manages the fund without owning it or what it owns. Whether it owns or lends is a relationship, not part of what it is.
_Avoid_: Lender, vehicle

**Rollup**:
A company that owns or operates many local practices, such as a dental support organization, a vet group, or a physician management company.
_Avoid_: DSO (for anything that is not dental), consolidator, platform

**Retirement plan**:
A public pension or an employer 401(k) plan that a user can check for exposure.
_Avoid_: Pension (for 401(k) plans), scheme

## Evidence

**Verified**:
Backed by two independent sources, or by one official filing.
_Avoid_: Confirmed, proven

**Reported**:
Backed by exactly one non-official source, and labeled that way to the reader.
_Avoid_: Unverified, alleged, single-source (in user-facing text)

**Sources disagree**:
The state of a fact whose sources contradict each other, such as two owners each said to hold a majority at the same time. It is not published as a fact; the reader sees that the sources disagree, with links to each. A brand's category reads sources disagree only when the readings would give it different categories.
_Avoid_: Disputed, conflict (in user-facing text)

**Checked**:
Said of a brand, company or location once every source the product requires for it has been searched or read, whether or not anything turned up. Its checked date is the date of the oldest of those searches, so the date never claims more than the stalest part of the check.
_Avoid_: Verified, researched, scanned

**Not yet checked**:
What a brand, company or location shows before its check is complete, unless private equity has already been found. Any owner found so far is still shown.
_Avoid_: Unknown, pending, unresearched

**Last confirmed**:
The most recent date any source still showed a fact. A hop not confirmed for a long stretch is shown with this date and stops counting as current ownership.
_Avoid_: Stale, expired, out of date (in user-facing text)

**Official filing**:
A record a government agency publishes or requires by law, such as an NPPES provider record, a Form 5500, or a Form ADV. One is enough to make a fact verified.
_Avoid_: Government source, authoritative source

**Independent sources**:
Two sources from different publishers' datasets or different web domains. A press release copied onto five sites is one source.
_Avoid_: Separate sources, multiple sources

**Registry**:
The curated list of PE firms and rollups and where their pages are. It tells the crawler where to look and is never itself evidence.
_Avoid_: Database, list of PE firms (as a source)

## Coverage

**Coverage state**:
One of the four labels every business visible at launch carries, saying how much the engine knows about its ownership. Always written in full, never as a bare "state".
_Avoid_: State (alone), status

**Launch state**:
A US state where the engine runs its per-state connectors (Secretary of State registry, veterinary license board) at launch.
_Avoid_: Seeded state, home state
