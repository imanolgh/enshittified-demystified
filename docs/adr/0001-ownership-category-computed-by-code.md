# Ownership category is computed by code from owner kind and the ownership chain

Status: accepted (2026-10-26). Supersedes the LLM `ownership_type` classification in `evidence_layer_plan.md` §4–§5 and `build_roadmap.md` Steps 7 and 27b.

A business's category (PE-owned, family-owned, No PE evidence found, etc.) is never chosen by the LLM. *(2026-11-07, engine "Category precedence and public companies": a category belongs to a brand, company or location; funds, firms, families and plans carry only an owner kind. Launch computes brands only.)* Each owner entity carries an **owner kind** (`private_equity, venture_capital, real_estate, other_investment_fund, conglomerate, family, employee_owned, trust, public_company, nonprofit, government, cooperative`; `cooperative` added 2026-11-07; `pe_firm` renamed `private_equity`, and `venture_capital` and `real_estate` added, 2026-11-07), and code computes each business's **category** by walking its ownership chains. We chose this because a category is a judgment no verbatim quote can prove, a per-link label stores the same owner fact many times so the copies can disagree, and reclassifying one owner must update every business beneath it automatically.

## Rules

- **Owner kind sources, ranked** *(amended 2026-10-26)*: (1) official filings (Form ADV, SEC filings/ticker, Form 5500 ESOP, IRS exempt-organization / Form 990) > (2) the firm's own words, quoted from its `company_site` or its own `press_release` > (3) third-party `news`, quoted. Ranks 2–3 are read by the LLM from a verbatim quote checked by `verify_citations` and publish as `reported`. A higher rank wins outright; a contradiction within the same rank gives "owner type not confirmed". The registry is a crawl list, never evidence, so it has no rank.
- **PE firm vs other funds:** `private_equity` = a Form ADV adviser with at least one fund of type "private equity fund". An adviser whose funds are only venture capital, hedge, or real estate is `other_investment_fund`, and a business it owns is `investment_fund_owned` — "PE-owned" never stretches to mean any investor.
  *(Amended 2026-11-07, engine "Owner kind: storage, evidence and the PE-firm test".)* Form ADV `Any PE Funds = Y` on an adviser is rank-1 evidence for `private_equity`, even for a firm that calls itself something else (Trian, 3G, BDT and JAB all report PE funds); `N` is not evidence of anything, because an affiliated adviser may report the funds (L Catterton's main US adviser says N). A self-description that maps to `private_equity` sets it as `reported`. `other_investment_fund` also covers credit funds; a BDC is `other_investment_fund` from its `814-` number, and its ticker doesn't count. Rank-1 sources per kind: `private_equity` Form ADV `Y`; `public_company` a ticker from an SEC filing (so a public conglomerate reads `public_company`); `nonprofit` the IRS exempt-org file; `employee_owned` Form 5500 ESOP (parked with retirement); a `family` entity is `family` by definition; `other_investment_fund`, `conglomerate`, `trust`, `government` and `cooperative` have no rank-1 source. Rank 2 means a document on one of the owner's own `domain` identifiers or a press release it issued; everything else is rank 3. Self-descriptions are mapped to kinds by a term table in code, never by a model; a pension fund maps to `other_investment_fund` and a sovereign wealth fund to `government`.
  *(Amended 2026-11-07, engine "Funds and their investment firm".)* The kind `pe_firm` is renamed `private_equity`, because a fund can carry it. Two kinds split out of `other_investment_fund`: `real_estate` and `venture_capital`, with the categories `real_estate_fund_owned` and `venture_capital_owned`. A firm with any PE fund is `private_equity`; a firm whose funds are all real estate or all venture capital takes that kind (from the Form ADV monthly counts by fund type); any other mix is `other_investment_fund`, which keeps hedge, credit, securitized-asset and liquidity funds. One investment firm is the firm as the public names it and may hold several adviser registrations; `Any PE Funds = Y` on any of them counts. A fund's own evidence decides its kind first: the Form ADV archive's per-fund `Fund Type` is rank 1 ("Private Equity Fund" → `private_equity`, "Real Estate Fund" → `real_estate`, "Venture Capital Fund" → `venture_capital`, hedge, securitized-asset and liquidity funds → `other_investment_fund`, "Other Private Fund" → nothing), and a BDC keeps its `814-` rule. A fund with no kind evidence takes the kind of the firm that manages it (`managed_by`), verified only if that edge and the firm's kind are both verified. Kind never flows up from a fund to its firm, or from a firm to a listed parent.
- **Chain walk:** walk up from the business until the first entity whose owner kind is `private_equity` (fund or firm), or the top. Unclassified intermediate owners do not block. PE anywhere in a chain beats the top owner's kind (a Blackstone fund under Blackstone Inc. is still PE). An unclassified top with no PE → "owner type not confirmed".
  *(Amended 2026-11-07, engine "Funds and their investment firm".)* A fund links to the firm that manages it by `managed_by`, which is never a hop. The walk stops at the fund or the firm, whichever the hop names. A fund and its firm, or two funds of one firm, count as one PE owner: hops to them never end each other and never count as sources disagreeing. A listed company above a firm (Blackstone Inc., The Goldman Sachs Group) stays a separate `public_company` linked by an ordinary hop, and the walk stops before reaching it.
- **Control vs minority:** a hop is control unless a quoted document states a stake under 50% or calls it a minority investment. Any minority hop makes that chain minority. A control chain to PE → `pe_owned`; only minority PE chains → `pe_minority_stake`, which still outranks every non-PE category (the card shows the controlling non-PE owner beside it).
  *(Amended 2026-11-07, engine "Category precedence and public companies".)* A stated voting stake decides control; an economic stake decides only when no voting stake is stated; a stake with no stated basis counts as voting. A public company with a PE holder above 50% of voting power reads PE-owned (Jersey Mike's, Chewy), with its ticker shown. Above a public company, a PE firm's minority stake counts only when it comes from a 13D, or from a 13G not filed under Rule 13d-1(b) (the box banks and fund managers tick for ordinary passive holdings, such as T. Rowe Price's 10.8% of Pacific Biosciences); proxy tables confirm a holder but never create the stake. A 13D or 13G amendment reporting 5% or less ends the holder's hop. Precedence: PE control, then PE minority. Among controlling co-owners the larger stated stake decides; with none stated, investment funds (venture capital, real estate, other), public company, conglomerate, employee-owned, trust, family, cooperative, nonprofit, government. Confirmed PE always shows; otherwise a controlling owner of unconfirmed kind gives `owner_type_not_confirmed`, and a minority hop to an unconfirmed owner changes nothing. `no_pe_evidence_found` is the floor.
- **Separation degree:** one stored row per (business, PE firm), counting hops to the first PE entity (direct ownership = 1; with several paths to one PE owner, the shortest, engine "Category precedence and public companies"); the card also names the firm without adding a hop. *(2026-11-07, engine "Funds and their investment firm": the row is keyed to the firm when the fund has a `managed_by` edge, else to the fund, so Jersey Mike's two Blackstone funds give one row; the degree counts to whichever PE entity comes first.)* Ownership hops only (`acquired_by, owned_by, operates_location_of`), never `lends_to`. *(Amended 2026-11-05, engine "Predicate semantics": `operates_location_of` is renamed `operated_by` (location → company); `acquired_by` and `owned_by` back one ownership hop, published as `owned_by`; `holds_position` and `allocates_to` are never hops.)* Recomputed whenever a hop or an owner kind is published, ended, or retracted.
- **Confidence:** a category is `verified` only if every hop and the deciding owner kind are verified; otherwise `reported`. *(2026-11-07, engine "Category precedence and public companies": with several chains, the category is verified when at least one chain that gives it is fully verified; other co-owners' chains don't lower it.)*
- **Time:** ~~a new controlling-owner claim ends the previous owner's hop (end date = new claim's date, with an `events` row).~~ *(Amended 2026-11-07, engine "Conflict, succession and co-investors": a hop ends only when (1) a document names that owner as the seller or the owner says it sold or exited, recorded as an `owned_by` claim with `holding: former`; (2) a completed acquisition states a stake that can't be true beside the owner's stated stake (a hop with no stated stake is never ended this way); or (3) the 18-month rule below. Every other overlap is co-ownership: THL and Linden both own Smile Doctors. Each ending writes an `events` row. The newest stake by as-of date decides control or minority, and a stated 0% is an exit. An undated claim takes its document's own date and never restarts a hop that a dated claim ended. Naming a firm as seller ends hops to the firm and all its funds; naming a fund ends only that fund's.)* A hop with no source confirming it for 18 months shows "last confirmed {date}" and stops counting as current. Ended PE ownership appears on the card as "formerly PE-owned {years}" history, not as a category.
- **Never "independent":** a business with no PE chain is `no_pe_evidence_found` (checked {date}). The only future route to the word is the Certified Independent badge (automated checks against official records) — **not in the MVP** (2026-10-26).

## Categories (`BusinessCategory`)

`pe_owned, pe_minority_stake, investment_fund_owned, venture_capital_owned, real_estate_fund_owned, conglomerate_owned, family_owned, employee_owned, trust_owned, public_company_owned, nonprofit_owned, government_owned, cooperative_owned, owner_type_not_confirmed, no_pe_evidence_found, sources_disagree` (`sources_disagree` applies only when contested edges lead to different categories; otherwise the category stands and the card's owner line says the sources disagree, engine "Conflict, succession and co-investors", 2026-11-07. `cooperative_owned` added 2026-11-07; `venture_capital_owned` and `real_estate_fund_owned` added 2026-11-07, engine "Funds and their investment firm"; precedence: engine "Category precedence and storage")

## Considered options

- **LLM classifies per document** (the original plan): best coverage, but unprovable, inconsistent across documents, and not repeatable.
- **Official sources only:** most defensible, but family/trust/conglomerate owners have no official marker, so most would be "not confirmed".
- **Label on each link:** already in the schema and one query to read, but duplicates the owner fact and cannot express "PE three hops up".
