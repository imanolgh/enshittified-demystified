## Question

What does someone have to do before they can post a note, and how strongly does the site keep bots out?

## Context

- Charted 2026-11-18. **Not worked until the developer unparks this map.**
- Developer: real people only, "no bots whatsoever". Claude's caution: no method keeps out every bot; email verification plus an invisible bot check stops most; phone or ID checks get close to none but add friction and hold personal data.
- Accounts are free and needed only to comment (account model; launch backend "Accounts: sign-in, stored data and deletion", which this waits on).
- Options to weigh: email verification; an invisible check at sign-up; phone verification; ID verification; limits for new accounts (first posts held, rate limits); usernames or pseudonyms; one account per person.
