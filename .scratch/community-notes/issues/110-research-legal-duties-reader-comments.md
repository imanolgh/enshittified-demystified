Status: open

## Question

What legal duties and protections apply to a one-person US website that hosts reader comments on pages naming companies, including comments about product quality and links?

## Context

- Charted 2026-11-18. **Not run until the developer unparks this map.**
- The site publishes ownership findings with quotes and receipts; notes are readers' views shown apart from those findings (map founding rule). Operator: Imanol Goikuria, self-funded ("The how-this-works page"); whether to form an LLC is open in chains "Legal review before launch".

## Find out

1. **Section 230** (47 U.S.C. § 230): what it covers for hosted comments, and what it doesn't (federal criminal law, intellectual property).
2. **DMCA safe harbor** (17 U.S.C. § 512): the designated-agent registration with the Copyright Office, notice-and-takedown, repeat-infringer policy.
3. **FTC:** endorsement guides as they apply to user comments and to affiliate links shown beside them.
4. **Children:** COPPA if under-13s could sign up; age gates.
5. **Other duties:** reporting obligations (e.g. child sexual abuse material to NCMEC under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A), state laws on content moderation or account deletion, and the EU Digital Services Act if EU residents can post.
6. Each point from the statute, regulation or agency page itself.
