# Research: How often consumer brands change owners

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none
Map: [non-pe-owners](../map.md)

## Question

Over the last 10 years, how often did the audited consumer brands change owners, how long did non-PE owners hold them before selling, which sources date those changes, and which listed parents of audited brands had activist holders filing 13Ds?

## Context

- Charted 2026-11-16. **Not run until the developer unparks this map.**
- The developer's case: "public companies that have short term interests and end up having no stake in the product 5 years out". Nature's Bounty is the example already in the glossary: Nestlé bought it in 2021 and agreed on 2026-09-01 to sell it to Yellow Wood Partners, closing expected by the first half of 2027 ([supplements](../../chains-and-brands/research/category-audit/supplements.md) SU2). After closing, the v1 card would show the PE years and Yellow Wood but not Nestlé's five years.
- Ownership changes the audits already found: three of 30 supplement rows changed owner in 2025–26 (The Vitamin Shoppe, vitafusion, Zarbee's) (supplements findings item 4). Kraft Heinz is the 3G case (3G reports PE funds on Form ADV, [category-audit-pilot](../../chains-and-brands/research/category-audit-pilot.md) §5).
- Activist holders the audits met: Trian (Form ADV `Any PE Funds = Y`, a holder in Wendy's), Pleasant Lake at Citi Trends (Y, 1 PE fund), Coliseum, GreenWood at a pilot restaurant, Arkhouse and a second group with 13Ds filed 2026-11-02 at Dine Brands, Hill Path at Dave & Buster's and Main Event, Icahn Enterprises at Monro (13D, no Form ADV) ([category-audit-full](../../chains-and-brands/research/category-audit-full.md); [industry-screen](../../chains-and-brands/research/industry-screen.md)).
- v1 rule above a public company: only a PE-kind firm's 13D, or a 13G not filed under Rule 13d-1(b), creates a minority stake; a 13D or 13G amendment reporting 5% or less ends the hop (ADR 0001).

## Find out

1. **Owner changes:** for the brands in the thirteen audited industries (or a stated sample if that's too many), every change of controlling owner since 2016, each with the buyer, seller, date and source, and the kind of each owner.
2. **Hold lengths:** how long each non-PE owner held a brand before selling or spinning it off, compared with PE holds in the same set. Spin-offs (a public company listing a division) counted separately.
3. **Dating sources:** which sources date a change (completion 8-Ks, buyer and seller releases, annual reports), and how often the only source is an announcement with no completion record.
4. **Activist holders:** for the listed parents of audited brands, every 13D filed since 2021 by an activist or investment firm: filer, stake, filing dates, the amendment that took it to 5% or less, and whether the filer reports PE funds on Form ADV.
