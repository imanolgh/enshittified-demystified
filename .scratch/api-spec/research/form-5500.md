# Research: Form 5500 bulk data contents

Ticket: [05-research-form-5500](../issues/05-research-form-5500.md) · Researched 2026-10-26 · Primary sources only (DOL/EBSA pages, the DOL datasets guide, per-file layout files on askebsa.dol.gov, and the 2024 Form 5500 instructions).

**Bottom line:** the structured bulk data does **not** contain a plan's fund-by-fund lineup. The Schedule H "Schedule of Assets (Held At End of Year)" (line 4i), where individual investments are listed, is a filer-prepared **attachment** (PDF or plain text). The CSVs carry only a Yes/No flag that the attachment exists. In structured form you get Schedule H/I/SF **aggregate dollar totals by asset class**, plus Schedule D Part I **names and EINs of the pooled vehicles** (CCTs, PSAs, MTIAs, 103-12 IEs) a plan invested in, with a dollar value for each. Mutual funds (registered investment companies) appear only as one aggregate dollar line, with no names.

## 1. Datasets published, URLs, formats, years, cadence, sizes

**Datasets per form year (2025 set, as listed on [S1]):** Form 5500; Form 5500-SF; 5500-SF Part 7; Schedule A (+ Part 1); Schedule C (+ Part 1 Items 1/2/3, Part 2, Part 3, and separate service-code tables); Schedule D (+ Part 1, Part 2); Schedule DCG (+ Plan Transfer); Schedule G (Parts 1–3); Schedule H (+ Question 5b); Schedule I (+ Question 5b); Schedule R (Parts 1–4 and 6; Part 5); Schedule MB (several sub-tables); Schedule MEP (+ Part 2); Schedule SB (several sub-tables). Later years add DCG, MEP and some MB/SB sub-tables that the 2021 list does not have. [S1]

**"All" vs "Latest":** for 2009 and later, each form year is published twice. [S2]
- "All": "includes all filings received by the Department for that form year and may contain multiple filings for a single plan due to amended or duplicate filings". Statuses: `FILING_RECEIVED`, `PROCESSING_STOPPED`, `FILING_ERROR`.
- "Latest": "includes only the latest filing received for a plan with a filing status of 'FILING_RECEIVED'".
- Before 2009, only an "All" set exists. [S1]

**Format:** "zipped CSV files – comma delimited … with "(double quote) as the text qualifier. The first row … contains the field names." Each CSV has a "File Layout" document listing field types (Text or Numeric). Dates are text in `mm/dd/yyyy`; `_AMT` fields typically have 2 decimal places; `_CNT` fields are whole numbers. Every dataset includes three system fields: filing status, date received, and a valid-signature indicator. [S2] Each year also has a Data Dictionary zip that maps fields to form line items, and recent years have a "Form Year Changes" xlsx. [S1][S2] When a field is removed from a form it stays in the layout, blank, to keep the structure stable across years. [S2]

**URL pattern:** `https://askebsa.dol.gov/FOIA%20Files/{YEAR}/{Latest|All}/{FILE}.zip`, with the layout at `.../{FILE}_layout.txt`. Filename case varies (e.g. `latest`/`Latest`), so build URLs from the page links rather than a fixed template. [S1] Observed 2026-10-26: `askebsa.dol.gov` answers with a "Document Moved" redirect to `www.askebsa.dol.gov`, so the HTTP client must follow redirects (curl `-L`).

**Years available:** 1999 through 2025. [S1] Older sets use different structures and data dictionaries (1999–2004 share the 1999 dictionary; 2005–2008 share the 2006 one). For 2008, Schedule SB/MB raw data "are not available". [S1] Anything before 1999, or a custom extract, requires a FOIA request and may cost a fee. [S1] From 2009 on, each dataset represents the **form year**; for 2008 and earlier it represents the plan year. [S1]

**Update cadence:** "2009 and later Form 5500 datasets are typically updated around the first of each month, give or take a few days"; the 2008 and earlier sets "are complete and will not change." [S1] Separately, filings show up on the public EFAST2 search within hours of receipt, but attachments can take up to 21 days. [S4, FAQ 43]

**File sizes (zip `Content-Length` from HTTP HEAD, 2026-10-26; all files last modified 2026-09-30):**

| File (form year 2024) | Zipped size |
|---|---|
| F_5500_2024_Latest | 28.6 MB |
| F_5500_2024_All | 30.5 MB |
| F_5500_SF_2024_Latest | 136.4 MB |

| F_SCH_D_PART1_2024_Latest | 8.7 MB |

Unzipped sizes and row counts were not measured.

**Joining:** "Each Form 5500/Form 5500-SF submission, and its schedules, is uniquely identified by an acknowledgement ID (ACK_ID field) … the best way to connect the data from a single filing submission to the various form and schedule tables." Other keys: `FORM_ID` identifies one schedule when a filing has several; `ROW_ORDER` identifies repeating rows; `CODE_ORDER` identifies Schedule C service codes. [S2]

## 2. Fields that could identify funds/investments; is line 4i structured?

**Line 4i (Schedule of Assets) is attachment-only. This is the key finding.**
- Schedule H line 4i: "Check 'Yes' if the plan had any assets held for investment purposes, and attach a schedule of assets held for investment purposes at end of year…" [S3, Schedule H instructions, Line 4i]
- The attached schedule must be labelled "Schedule H, line 4i – Schedule of Assets (Held At End of Year)". Its columns are (a) party-in-interest asterisk, (b) identity of issue/borrower/lessor, (c) description of investment, (d) cost, (e) current value. A second schedule, "Acquired and Disposed of Within Year", is also required. [S3, Schedule H instructions, "Line 4i schedules"]
- Attachments in general: "At the top of each attachment, indicate the schedule and line, if any (e.g., Schedule H, line 4i) to which the attachment relates." [S3, General Instructions]
- EFAST2 accepts attachments as "PDF or TXT (plain text format)"; a few items must be PDF. [S4, FAQ 27]
- The Schedule H dataset layout has **no** fields for issue names or holdings. Its only line-4i field is the indicator `AST_HELD_INVST_IND`. [L-H]
- Further limits on the attachment itself: participant-directed brokerage-window assets "must be treated as one asset" on the 4i schedule, and cost (column d) "may be omitted" for participant-directed investments. [S3, Schedule H instructions, Notes to line 4i schedules]

**Structured fields that do relate to investments:**
- **Schedule H, Part I (large plans, aggregate dollars only, beginning and end of year)** [L-H]:
  - `INT_COMMON_TR_*` (CCTs, line 1c(9))
  - `INT_POOL_SEP_ACCT_*` (PSAs)
  - `INT_MASTER_TR_*` (MTIAs)
  - `INT_103_12_INVST_*` (103-12 IEs)
  - `INT_REG_INVST_CO_*` (registered investment companies, i.e. mutual funds, closed-end funds and UITs, line 1c(13) [S3])
  - `INS_CO_GEN_ACCT_*`
  - `OTH_INVST_*`
  - `JOINT_VENTURE_*`, `CORP_DEBT_*`, `COMMON_STOCK_*`, `EMPLR_SEC_*`, `OTHER_LOANS_*`, `PARTCP_LOANS_*`, etc.

  Part II also has matching gain/loss lines. These are totals by asset class. They do not name any fund.
- **Schedule D Part I (names the pooled vehicles)** [L-D1]: `DFE_P1_ENTITY_NAME` (text, 35 chars), `DFE_P1_SPONS_NAME` (70), `DFE_P1_PLAN_EIN` (9), `DFE_P1_PLAN_PN` (3), `DFE_P1_ENTITY_CODE` (M/C/P/E), `DFE_P1_PLAN_INT_EOY_AMT` (the plan's dollar interest at year end), keyed by `ACK_ID` + `ROW_ORDER`.
  - Part I is "required for a plan or DFE that invested or participated in any MTIAs, 103-12 IEs, CCTs, and/or PSAs". [S3, General Instructions] It lists "all MTIAs, CCTs, PSAs, and 103-12 IEs in which the plan or DFE … participated at any time during the plan or DFE year". [S3, Schedule D Part I]
  - The EIN/PN must match the DFE's own Form 5500. If the CCT or PSA filed no Form 5500, the filer enters the vehicle's EIN with PN `000`. [S3, Schedule D element (c)]
  - **Mutual funds are not on Schedule D.** Only MTIA, CCT, PSA and 103-12 IE are valid entity types. [S3]
  - The 35-character name field may truncate long CCT names. This is inferred from the layout width and was not observed in data.
- **Schedule D Part II** (filed by a DFE) lists the plans that invest in that DFE: `DFE_P2_PLAN_NAME`, `DFE_P2_PLAN_SPONS_NAME`, `DFE_P2_PLAN_EIN`, `DFE_P2_PLAN_PN`. [L-D2] Part II "is required when the Form 5500 is filed for a DFE, except DCGs". [S3] This gives a reverse map from a CCT/PSA/MTIA to the plans invested in it, but only where the vehicle files as a DFE.
- **Schedule C** names service providers (`PROVIDER_OTHER_NAME`, EIN, service codes, and direct and indirect compensation), e.g. recordkeepers, investment managers and trustees. It does not name investment options. [L-C12][S2]
- **Schedule H trust fields:** `FDCRY_TRUST_NAME`, `FDCRY_TRUST_EIN`, `FDCRY_TRUSTEE_CUST_NAME`. [L-H]

## 3. 5500-SF filers, DC-plan codes, amendments, EIN+PN key, sponsor fields

**5500-SF (small plans): no investment detail at all.**
- Eligibility: generally fewer than 100 participants at the beginning of the plan year, and the plan must also be exempt from the IQPA audit requirement, have "100 percent of its assets invested in certain secure investments with a readily determinable fair market value", hold no employer securities, not be a multiemployer plan, and so on. [S3, Quick Reference Chart note 2] The 80–120 participant rule lets plans in that range keep filing in their prior category. [S3, General Instructions]
- The SF layout has only totals: `SF_TOT_ASSETS_BOY/EOY_AMT`, `SF_NET_ASSETS_*`, contributions, income, expenses, `SF_PARTCP_LOANS_EOY_AMT`, `SF_ELIGIBLE_ASSETS_IND`. It has no asset-class breakdown and no Schedule D. [L-SF] The SF file is the largest one (136 MB zipped for 2024), which is consistent with most plans by count being small.
- Small plans that file the full Form 5500 attach **Schedule I**. Schedule I is also aggregate only (totals plus a few asset-type indicator/amount pairs such as `SMALL_JOINT_VENTURE_EOY_*`, `SMALL_EMPLR_SEC_EOY_*`, `SMALL_20_PRCNT_SNGL_INVST_*`). [L-I] The schedule of assets is tied to Schedule H line 4i: "Schedules of assets … also must be filed … if Schedule H, line 4i or 4j is 'Yes'". [S3, Quick Reference Chart note 5]

**DC-plan filter codes:** `TYPE_PENSION_BNFT_CODE` (Form 5500) and `SF_TYPE_PENSION_BNFT_CODE` (5500-SF) are 40-character text fields that hold the filer's concatenated plan-characteristic codes. [L-5500][L-SF] Relevant DC codes from [S3] (Form 5500 line 8a code list):
- 2A age/service-weighted or new-comparability allocation
- 2B target benefit
- 2C money purchase
- 2E profit-sharing
- 2F ERISA 404(c)
- 2G total participant-directed
- 2H partial participant-directed
- 2J **Code section 401(k) feature**
- 2K 401(m)
- 2L 403(b)(1) annuity
- 2M 403(b)(7) custodial
- 2R brokerage window
- 2S automatic enrollment
- 2T default investment account (QDIA)

1x codes cover defined-benefit features (e.g. 1I frozen plan). The 5500-SF layout also has `SF_401K_PLAN_IND`. [L-SF] Exact parsing rules for the concatenated field (separator, ordering) are not stated in the sources read; see the open list below.

**Amendments:**
- "An amended filing should be submitted as a complete replacement of the previously submitted filing", with all schedules and attachments. [S4, FAQ 4]
- The data carries `AMENDED_IND`, `INITIAL_FILING_IND`, `FINAL_FILING_IND`, `SHORT_PLAN_YR_IND`, `FILING_STATUS` and `DATE_RECEIVED`. [L-5500]
- "Latest" keeps only the latest `FILING_RECEIVED` filing per plan. [S2]
- Filing statuses: "Processing Stopped" and "Filing Error" filings count as received but are incomplete or erroneous. [S4, FAQ 38]

**Plan key (EIN+PN):** PN is "a unique 12-digit number to identify the plan" when combined with the line 2b EIN, and is "used by the IRS, DOL, and PBGC". A number, once used, stays with that plan and is never reused, "even if the first plan or DFE is terminated". Pension plans and DFEs number from 001; welfare-only plans number from 501. [S3, Line 1b]
- Fields: `SPONS_DFE_EIN` + `SPONS_DFE_PN` (Form 5500); `SF_SPONS_EIN` + `SF_PLAN_NUM` (SF); `SCH_H_EIN`/`SCH_H_PN` and similar on schedules. [L-5500][L-SF][L-H]
- For a DFE, line 2b holds the EIN of the CCT/PSA/MTIA/103-12 IE/DCG/GIA itself, not an employer's EIN. [S3, Line 2b] `TYPE_DFE_PLAN_ENTITY_CD` separates DFE filings from plan filings. [L-5500]
- Changes of sponsor or plan number are signalled through `LAST_RPT_SPONS_NAME`, `LAST_RPT_SPONS_EIN`, `LAST_RPT_PLAN_NUM` and `LAST_RPT_PLAN_NAME`. [L-5500]
- `ACK_ID` identifies a single *submission*, not a plan. [S2]

**Employer (sponsor) identification fields (Form 5500):** [L-5500]
- `SPONSOR_DFE_NAME` (70), `SPONS_DFE_DBA_NAME`, `SPONS_DFE_CARE_OF_NAME`
- mailing address and location address (`SPONS_DFE_MAIL_US_*`, `SPONS_DFE_LOC_US_*`)
- `SPONS_DFE_EIN`, `SPONS_DFE_PHONE_NUM`
- `BUSINESS_CODE` (6 characters, the line 2d principal business activity code)
- `PLAN_NAME` (140)
- administrator (`ADMIN_*`), preparer (`PREPARER_*`)
- participant counts (`TOT_ACTIVE_PARTCP_CNT`, `PARTCP_ACCOUNT_BAL_CNT`, etc.)

The 5500-SF has equivalent `SF_SPONSOR_NAME`, `SF_SPONS_*`, `SF_BUSINESS_CODE` and `SF_PLAN_NAME`. [L-SF] Multiemployer plans list contributing employers in Schedule R Part I (`PEN_CONTRIB_EMPLR_NAME`, `PEN_CONTRIB_EMPLR_EIN`). [L-R1]

## 4. Anything resembling a "sponsor index"

- DOL publishes **no** separate employer→plan index file. The equivalent is built by the consumer from the Form 5500 and 5500-SF tables (sponsor name, DBA, EIN, and address → EIN+PN + plan name). [S1][S2] (Absence inferred: no such dataset appears in the [S1] listing.)
- **EFAST2 Form 5500 Search** (https://www.efast.dol.gov/5500Search/, also linked as https://5500search.dol.gov/) "provides the forms, schedules, and attachments submitted by the filer". It shows filings received by EFAST2 for plan years 2009 and later. You can "view one plan filing at a time", filter by plan type, asset value and participant count, get up to 5,000 results, and export results to CSV. [S5] **This is the only DOL route to the line-4i Schedule of Assets attachments.** No bulk download or documented API for attachments was found.
- EFAST2 "publicly shows successfully submitted Form 5500, 5500-SF, and PR filings", except filings with sensitive information, plan years before 2009, and one-participant and foreign plans. Form 5500-EZ is never published. [S4, FAQ 42]
- DOL also publishes cleaned "Form 5500 Research Datafiles" used for EBSA's bulletins (per the data.gov catalog: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/form-5500-research-datafiles). Contents were not examined.

## Implications for the design (facts and consequences only)

- Scenario B1a as written (research_flows.md:123–126) assumes bulk CSVs give a per-plan fund lineup for `allocates_to`. **The structured data does not support that for mutual funds.** Mutual-fund holdings are one aggregate dollar figure per plan (`INT_REG_INVST_CO_EOY_AMT`).
- From structured data alone, a large plan can yield:
  - named `allocates_to`-like relations to CCTs, PSAs, MTIAs and 103-12 IEs (Schedule D Part I: name, EIN/PN, end-of-year dollar value)
  - asset-class totals (Schedule H)

  A small plan (5500-SF or Schedule I) yields totals only.
- A true fund-by-fund lineup requires fetching per-filing line 4i attachments (PDF/TXT, free-form within the prescribed columns) from EFAST2 one filing at a time. That is document retrieval plus table parsing/extraction, not a bulk load. Because the columns are prescribed, a parser could target them, but layouts are filer-prepared. Attachments also lag the structured data by up to 21 days.
- Line-4i quirks affect any exposure number built from it:
  - brokerage windows collapse to one line
  - cost may be blank
  - CCT holdings are opaque even when named (the look-through would need the CCT's own DFE filing, if one exists)
- "Free headline exposure number" (pre-launch-seeding.md:71): what the bulk data can mechanically support is aggregate exposure by asset class (e.g. share of assets in CCTs, PSAs, 103-12 IEs, insurance general accounts, or "other investments"), not exposure to named private-credit funds.
- Bulk claims would carry provenance as (dataset file, form year, Latest/All, ACK_ID, ROW_ORDER). The plan's stable identity is EIN+PN; ACK_ID changes with every amendment.
- The "Latest" set is replaced roughly monthly, and a plan's row can change when it amends. Re-ingest has to tolerate superseded ACK_IDs, which matters for append-only claims and idempotency.
- A DFE filing has a vehicle's EIN in the sponsor-EIN field. Filtering on `TYPE_DFE_PLAN_ENTITY_CD` / `TYPE_PLAN_ENTITY_CD` keeps CCTs etc. from being treated as employers.
- The sponsor index for B2 can be built from F_5500 + F_5500_SF (name/DBA/EIN/address/BUSINESS_CODE → EIN+PN). Plans that file the 5500-EZ (e.g. one-participant plans) are not public. [S4]
