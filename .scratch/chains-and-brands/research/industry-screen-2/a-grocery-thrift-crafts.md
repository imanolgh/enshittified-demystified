Saved pages (NNN.html raw, NNN.txt visible text), `index.tsv`, `robots-log.tsv` and scripts are in the session scratchpad under `pages/screen2-a/` and `scripts/screen2-a/` (temporary).

Every ownership fact comes from a primary source: SEC filings, company or sponsor releases, sponsor portfolio pages, company sites, Form ADV. One row (Paper Source) rests on Elliott's release as reprinted by STT/Business Wire on sttinfo.fi (businesswire.com denied access), with the sale's completion from a deal adviser's release (The CMA Group), flagged where it appears. Robots.txt was read for every host before the first request (and after every redirect), for `*`, `EvidenceEngine` and Claude's agents.

Counting: PE-owned = control, or a PE hop with no stated stake. Public companies: PE-owned when a PE holder has over 50% of the votes; PE minority only from a 13D or a 13G not filed under Rule 13d-1(b), from a firm whose Form ADV says `Any PE Funds = Y`. Rows are the store names consumers see; when the ranking is by company, the row is the company's lead banner (its namesake, or the banner it names first), with its other banners in the note. Sensitivities for other row rules are given with each share.
