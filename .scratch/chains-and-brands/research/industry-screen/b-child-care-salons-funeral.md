## Quote check, robots and cost

- 64 of 64 quotes matched the saved page text (whitespace, curly quotes, dashes and zero-width characters normalized); 3 deliberately altered quotes all failed. Two cited items are page fields (TSG's "Investment Year 2022 / Status Current", Fantastic Sams' HQ line).
