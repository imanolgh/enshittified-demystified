## Quote check, robots, surprises and cost

- 62 of 62 quotes matched the saved page text (whitespace, curly quotes, dashes and zero-width characters normalized); 4 altered quotes failed. Field-style quotes: Harvest's "Status Current" and "Year of Investment June 2022", LGP's "Status: Current", Seidler's "Status Active", Beiersdorf's page title, YouGov's CSV rows. Drybar 200+ and Elements 240+ came from search results only and weren't used as sizes.
- **Robots slips** (reported by the worker; all allowed after the fact): before the fetcher was patched it followed same-site redirects inside a 10-second crawl-delay on harvestpartners.com and transomcap.com, and followed four cross-host redirects before reading the new host's robots.txt (www.jergens.com → us.jergens.com, www.handandstone.com → handandstone.com, www.elementsmassage.com → elementsmassage.com, amazinglashstudio.com → www); all four robots.txt files were read afterwards and each allows the bot. The fetcher then refused cross-host redirects to unread hosts. Blocked or unreadable: massageenvy.com (Incapsula challenge; one browser render, no clicks, came back empty); wellbizbrands.com, drybar.com and Morningstar (403); Brand Finance's full table (needs clicks). SEC: 44 requests under 2 per second.
- Surprises: Gold Bond is not PE-owned (Sanofi kept it out of the Opella sale to CD&R); European Wax Center went private on 2026-05-08 under General Atlantic; WellBiz moved from KSL to Transom in January 2026 (Castanea's "Drybar" listing looks stale); Ideal Image appears defunct; Kimberly-Clark closed its Kenvue purchase on 2026-11-02; no free brand-level US sales ranking exists for beauty or personal care.
- Cost: about 45–50 minutes; 97 logged fetches (89 OK, 3 refused with 403, 1 not found, 1 cross-host redirect refused, 3 browser renders), 51 robots.txt reads, about 27 web searches; roughly 200k tokens.
