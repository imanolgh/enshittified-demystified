Ownership facts from SEC filings, company sites and sponsor pages; rankings from trade press. Page texts, the fetch log, the quote file and scripts were kept in the session scratchpad (temporary).

**Headline:** none of the three needs a full audit at top-10 level. Packaged food 0 of 10 company rows PE; beverages 0 of 9 sourced rows (Heineken not found); pet food 2 of 10 company rows PE-owned, but only 1 of the 8 rows that sell a consumer brand. **Method note:** all three free rankings rank companies, not brands, so each row is a company with its lead US brands and the PE share is by company row. Counting each brand separately would weight the lists further toward the big public and family owners.
