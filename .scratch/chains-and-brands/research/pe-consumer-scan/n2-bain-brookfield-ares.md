- **Quote check:** 42 of 42 match (whitespace, curly quotes, dashes and NFKC normalized); 3 of 3 altered controls fail.
- **Robots, blocks and slips:** robots.txt read first, in separate steps, for 16 hosts. Bain, Brookfield www, Henkel and Varsity allowed; Ares allowed except query strings, `.json`, `.pdf` and `.xml` other than sitemaps (Python's parser reads only the first `User-agent: *` group, so the merged rules were checked by hand; every Ares request complied); ir.ares.com allows all; tools.cms-eqs.com and data.sec.gov 404 (no restrictions). Brookfield's bbuc, bam and bn subdomains answered robots.txt with 403; one page request to bbuc.brookfield.com got Cloudflare's "Sorry, you have been blocked", treated as a refusal, not retried; bam and bn got nothing beyond robots.txt. efts.sec.gov and www.unilever.com also answered robots.txt with 403 and weren't used. **Slip:** 6 GET requests to the Ares content API (tools.cms-eqs.com) in one loop at about 1 per second; the first answered 401 (needs a token) and the loop didn't stop, so 5 more 401s followed; no retry, no attempt to get a token; the page's own UI was used instead. **Judgment calls:** Bain listing data from 165 POSTs to the site's own `/bcajaxdata` endpoint (not disallowed by robots.txt); the Ares list paged by clicking its own "Next" button 65 times (ordinary pagination, not a challenge); browser renders also loaded the pages' own third-party calls (Google Analytics, a reCAPTCHA anchor, the content API). Reused the parent's Bain sitemap downloads and another worker's SEC ticker file. No crawl-delays set; every request at least 1.1 s apart per host (SEC 0.6 s).
- **What the method misses:** newsrooms are selective (Bain about 30 PE-type releases a year, far fewer than its deals; Ares has no release for the 2019 Savers purchase or for consumer holdings known from other sources such as Smart & Final, 99 Cents Only, Guitar Center, Floor & Decor; deals before 2015 still held are invisible, e.g. Bain's Bob's Discount Furniture, Canada Goose, Virgin Voyages); exits are almost never announced (Bain's newsroom has an exit release for 0 of 26 kept companies), so "current" overstates; Brookfield is title-only (text on blocked subdomains; thin before 2021); the filter is title-only (untagged Bain titles read by hand; an Ares noise-dropping regex may have dropped a deal); consumer-facing calls and labels are judgment; stake sizes, the Epic-to-Aveanna link and the Gong cha closing not verified.
- **Cost:** about 50 minutes; about 380 logged HTTP requests (202 Bain, 138 Brookfield www, 15 ir.ares.com, 4 ares.com, 6 cms-eqs, 11 SEC, 2 Varsity, 1 bbuc), 16 robots.txt reads and 10 browser sessions (one paged the Ares list, whose own scripts made about 65 API calls); roughly 170k tokens.
