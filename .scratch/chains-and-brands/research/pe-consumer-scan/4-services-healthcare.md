## 3. Notes

- Not counted: Sentinel (403); Valor (no current/exited split); three undescribed Garnett companies.
- Domains corrected: greenbriar.com, tscp.com, lorientcap.com, fflpartners.com (the hosts tried first didn't resolve or had TLS errors).
- Surprises: healthcare-provider rollups are the largest group outside hotels (seven of the 22 firms are healthcare specialists, so this firm set over-weights healthcare); infusion therapy (6 platforms) and longevity clinics were unexpected; KSL's 32 hotels mostly operate under franchisor brands (Ritz-Carlton, Westin, Hyatt), so the brand consumers see isn't the PE owner's; home services are nearly all local-name rollups; Garnett is almost all restaurant franchisors and franchisees plus car washes, gyms and pet stores.
- Shared names: Kona Ice (Seidler and Garnett), Woof Gang (Great Hill and Garnett), CenExel (Webster and BayPine, B2B). Shore's page records selling Fast Pace to Revelstoke.
- Ambiguous statuses followed as written: LTP ("Partial Exit"), RedBird's YES Network and Go Rentals (partially realized); Shore's "(Merged)" entries left out.
- Cost: about 48 minutes; 90 page requests plus 34 robots.txt fetches, at most 1 request per second per host (10 seconds on Webster); 3 browser visits; roughly 200k tokens (harness count 239k).
