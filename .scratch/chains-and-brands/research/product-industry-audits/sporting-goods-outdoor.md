- **The list is a composite built from sources that know nothing about ownership** (see "Ranking"): NRF's Top 100 Retailers 2026 for the store chains, then Walmart's own "Best Sellers in Sports & Outdoors" shelf and its grills page sorted by best seller for the product brands. - **Biggest finding:** the result sits exactly on the bar and depends on three judgment calls the developer should make (open questions 1–3): whether KT Tape is sporting goods, whether a deal close reported only by a law firm and trade press counts, and how FountainVest's 13D ages under the 18-month rule.

- **PE sits in mid-size outdoor platforms, as the screen found:** Centre Partners' Gathr Outdoors (GCI Outdoor, Klymit, Rightline Gear and others), Charlesbank's Bridges (KT Tape), and BDT's grill and fishing holdings (Weber–Blackstone, and per trade press Rather Outdoors with Zebco).

## How this was sourced (read first)

- **Ranking:** composite (see "Ranking"). NRF's 2026 Top 100 list for chains; Walmart's best-seller pages for product brands. Rejected or blocked candidates are listed there. Ranking sources were never used as ownership evidence.
- **Ownership facts** come from SEC filings (submissions data, Schedule 13D/13G cover pages, an 8-K exhibit), sponsor releases and portfolio pages (Centre Partners, Charlesbank), company releases (Bass Pro, Walmart, Greif, Blackstone), company brand pages (Amer Sports, Newell), and brand footers, about pages and terms.
- **SEC access:** 32 requests to SEC hosts (www.sec.gov, data.sec.gov, efts.sec.gov) plus 3 robots.txt reads, all with the user agent `EvidenceEngine ownership-research bot (product industry audit)`, no contact detail, at least 1.1 s apart per host and never in parallel; all returned HTTP 200 (data.sec.gov's robots.txt was 404 and efts.sec.gov's 403, both "no restrictions" under the rule).
- **13D/13G method:** for each public parent (Dick's, Academy, Amer Sports, Newell, Walmart, Greif), every 13D/13G filing since 2024-01-01 was listed from the company's SEC submissions data. Filers were identified by EDGAR full-text search (Dick's, Academy) or by the accession-number prefix of known passive filers (Vanguard 0000102909, 0002100119, 0002100121; BlackRock 0002012383, 0002052113, 0001086364; FMR 0000315066; State Street 0000093751; Wellington 0000902219; Dimensional 0000354204; Managed Account Advisors 0001418660). Every 13D and every 13G whose filer was not one of those was opened and its cover read (filer, rule box, percent). Gap: a 13G filed through a shared agent under one of those prefixes would be read as that agent's client.
- **Robots:** robots.txt was read (by script, before the first request to each host, including after every redirect) for 71 hosts, obeying `*`, `EvidenceEngine`, ClaudeBot, Claude-User, Claude-SearchBot and anthropic-ai, and Crawl-delay. Crawl-delay 10 was obeyed on about.basspro.com, greif.com and intexcorp.com.
- **Blocked or failed:**
  - Robots disallow (not fetched): lek.com (ClaudeBot and anthropic-ai; the L.E.K. Brand Heat Index), amazon.com best sellers (Claude agents), gcioutdoor.com (anthropic-ai), ratheroutdoors.com (all agents), dometicgroup.com investor pages (ClaudeBot, anthropic-ai), supplierone.helpdocs.io (Walmart supplier docs; all agents), igloocoolers.rmaffiliate.com (all agents).
  - Refused: dickssportinggoods.com (403 "Site Unavailable", not a challenge; robots.txt also 403), basspro.com and rei.com (robots.txt 403; no page requested), academy.com (307 redirect to a captcha page; not retried because no row needed it), omm.com (403 "Just a moment..." challenge on a law firm's release; not retried in the browser because a law firm's release isn't an approved source type).
  - 404: amer.weber.com (the Weber–Blackstone closing release; the old weber.com URL now redirects to the homepage), strikeking.com (a Rather Outdoors release), dometic.com investors page.
  - JavaScript shells with no content: target.com category page, frasers.group.
- **Never fetched:** competitor sites, Reddit, WhoIsMyDentist, Private Equity Vet; PitchBook, Preqin, CB Insights, Mergr, MergerLinks and privateequityinfo results that came up in searches.
- **Leads only, never cited:** trade press on deals (Tackle Trade World, Wired2Fish, PE Hub, Hartford Business, SGB, Cookout News, RV Pro), Wikipedia and mirrors, trademark mirrors (Justia, trademarkelite), affiliate-network listings (FlexOffers, Affilitizer, LinkClicky), aggregator shareholder tables (Simply Wall St, TipRanks).
- **Owner kinds are research notes, not engine classifications.** "PE-owned" means control or a PE owner with no stated stake. "Private, owner not stated" means a primary source names a company other than the brand itself, or its founder, but not who owns it. "Not found" means the pages read show only the brand's own name.
- **Quotes:** copied from saved page text (tags stripped, whitespace collapsed, entities decoded); each quote line carries its page-cache tag `[pNNN]` (`NNN.txt` and `NNN.html` under the scratchpad's `pages/sporting-goods/`, listed in `index.tsv`). No quote came through WebFetch (it was not used). Values read from XML or JSON (13D/G percents, Walmart item names) are matched against the saved raw file.

## Ranking

**No free, ownership-blind ranking of US sporting goods brands that reaches 30 was found.** Checked and rejected:
- **L.E.K. 2026 Brand Heat Index, "Outdoor equipment & sporting goods"** (consumer survey, about 650 brands): lek.com disallows ClaudeBot and anthropic-ai. The trade write-ups read (SGB, SGI Europe) name only the top two (Stanley, YETI) and carry the top 10 as an image [p002][p003].
- **Similarweb, "Best Selling Sports & Outdoors Brands on Amazon"** [p001]: the free table shows 5 brands sorted by share change, not share (Owala, CAP Barbell, Mikolo, Meta, HydroJug), and the sub-category figures look like placeholders for non-subscribers.
- **YouGov Ratings**: no sporting goods or outdoor brand category [p009].
- **Amazon best sellers** (robots disallow), **Dick's, REI, Bass Pro, Academy** (refused or captcha), **Target** (client-rendered, no products in HTML) [p011], **Scheels** (robots disallow the sort parameter).
- **Walmart's department page sorted by best seller** [p012]: rejected for noise (drink mixes, an Apple Watch, trading cards and marketplace items in the top 40); the curated best-seller shelf was used instead.
- **The earlier screen's composite** (SEC revenue for public companies): biased against private and PE-owned companies, which file no revenue.
