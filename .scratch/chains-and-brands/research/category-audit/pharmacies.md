- **The category is mostly public companies and family or employee owners.** Walgreens is the one PE-owned brand, but it is the #2 US pharmacy by prescription revenue (12.1% of all US dispensing revenue, per DCI).
- **Fewer than 30 national chains exist.** Rows 1–8 are every retail-storefront entry in the DCI top 15. Rows 9–30 are a composite of franchise networks and large regional chains (see Ranking). The list is not exhaustive.
- **Three live ownership changes:**
  - CVS Health plans to split into two public companies; the retail company will be named CVS Pharmacy, expected in the first half of 2028 (10-Q, 2026-11-04).
  - Kroger agreed on 2026-07-01 to buy Giant Eagle; closing is expected in 2027 (8-K).
  - Rite Aid has closed all its stores and liquidated (DCI). It is not a row.
