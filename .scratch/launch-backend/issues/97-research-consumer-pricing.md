Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: none
Map: [launch-backend](../map.md)

## Question

What do comparable consumer products charge, for what, and how do they split free from paid?

## Context

- Charted 2026-11-18. **Not run until the developer unparks this map.**
- `monetizationideas.md` priced alerts for the local-businesses era ($19–29/yr insurance framing; Premium about $8/mo or $79/yr; founding price about $5/mo or $49/yr). The pivot proposal suggested about $5/mo or $49/yr for alerts (decisions.md §14). Buy'r reported about $100K in annual recurring revenue within 12 days (self-reported, company press release; pivot handoff).
- Competitor sites are leads a person reads, never sources of ownership facts (chains map Notes); their own pricing pages are fine as sources for their prices.

## Find out

1. **Products:** Buy'r, Ground News, Yuka, Goods Unite Us, the Worse on Purpose newsletter, Ethos, and consumer alert or monitoring services with rare events (credit and identity monitoring, price-drop alerts).
2. **For each:** prices (monthly, yearly, lifetime), what's free, what's paid, trial or beta terms, founding or early-supporter offers, and how the paid tier is described.
3. Each fact from the product's own pricing or help pages or its press releases, dated.
