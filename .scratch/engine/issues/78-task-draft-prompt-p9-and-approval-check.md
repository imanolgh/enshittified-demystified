- **What p9 is:** the v8 test prompt plus the rules from "Extraction prompt rules" (Q1 to Q5), and four rulings made during this check:
  - On a company's own site, in its own voice only, "we", "us" and "our company" name the site's owner. This does not apply to quoted speech or to a press release posted on the site (developer).
  - A DSO, or anyone else, providing services or support to offices or practices does not own their brands. The provider counts as the owner only when the document says it owns or operates them, or lists them as its own brands (developer).
  - Locations "owned and operated by" local dentists, doctors or entrepreneurs are covered by the franchise rule.
  - An agreed sale that has not closed is not an exit.
- **How Claude implemented the Q4 "affiliate" rule:** "affiliate" counts only for an owner or seller named as an affiliate of Y, or for a company formed for a deal and defined in a deal document ("Parent", "Merger Sub", "Buyer", "Purchaser"). These are the developer's own examples. The documents give no other way to tell Ashley's "Ashley Home, Inc., an affiliate of Ashley Global Retail, LLC, will acquire" apart from "Parent and Merger Sub are affiliates of Bansk Group". This reading gives up Banfield's "an Affiliate of Mars", which is a true relationship.
