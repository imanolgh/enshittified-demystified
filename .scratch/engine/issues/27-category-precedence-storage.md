# Category precedence and storage

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 14, 25, 65  (Owner kind: storage, evidence and the PE-firm test; Conflict, succession and co-investors; Funds and their investment firm)
Map: [engine](../map.md)

## Question

What is the full precedence among business categories, which entity types get a category, and are categories (and separation degrees) stored or computed on read?

## Context

- Only two precedence rules exist: PE beats non-PE; minority PE beats non-PE (ADR 0001:11–12,20; ADR 0002:11). No order among non-PE categories, nor for sources_disagree / owner_type_not_confirmed vs a verified PE chain on another path.
- **Storage**: ADR 0001:13 implies a stored (business, PE firm) row with separation degree; columns undefined. Is the category itself stored (with checked_at and the deciding chain) or computed on read? Recompute bookkeeping after an owner-kind change (async fan-out above N businesses). Where does formerly-PE history live? (evidence_layer_plan.md:194; build_roadmap.md:61,73)
- **Which entity types get a category**: businesses only, or rollups and brands too (an A2 search for a DSO)?
- Several chains to the same PE firm → separation degree = minimum (default).
- **From "What a business entity is"** (2026-11-05, [ADR 0005](../../../docs/adr/0005-brands-companies-locations-separate.md)): the candidate types are now `brand`, `company` and `location` (no `business` or `rollup`). The franchise rule needs a brand verdict and an operator verdict, so more than one type may need a category.
- **From "Schema conventions"** (2026-11-05; conventions in evidence_layer_plan.md §4; no on-demand research per decisions.md §15): the "A2 search for a DSO" example now means a search for a seeded rollup company; searches never start research.
- **From "Predicate semantics"** (2026-11-05; role table in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): a store has two verdicts: the brand verdict walks brand → owner; the store verdict walks location → `operated_by` → operator → owners. Hops are `owned_by` and `operated_by`; `lends_to`, `holds_position` and `allocates_to` never are. A brand's chain may skip the operating company, so separation degree can be one lower than reality.
- **From chains-and-brands "Brand and chain model"** (2026-11-06): at launch only brands are looked up, so categories are needed for brands. Store-map pins reuse the brand's verdict, so per-store categories aren't needed until operators arrive. The glossary's category entries still say "business"; rewording them to the entities that carry a category is this ticket's.
- **From "Claim value schemas"** (2026-11-06; schemas in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): ownership claims may carry `stake_basis` (`voting|economic`) beside `stake_pct`; how the basis feeds ADR 0001's "under 50%" rule (Jersey Mike's: Blackstone ~76.5% voting) is this ticket's. Only `acquired_by` claims with `deal_status = completed` back a hop.
- **Moved from "Owner kind"** (2026-11-06 split): It also raised whether a public company that a PE fund controls (Jersey Mike's) and a public company with a PE-fund 5%+ holder count as PE-owned and PE minority stake (§9). Public companies today read "Public company (NYSE: CMG)"; whether a PE-controlled public company reads PE-owned, and whether a PE fund's 5%+ stake in a public company makes it PE minority stake, are this ticket's.
- **From "Owner kind: storage, evidence and the PE-firm test"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 amended; table in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `owner_kind_evidence`): new category `cooperative_owned` (owner kind `cooperative`). `entities.owner_kind_confidence` (`verified` for rank 1, `reported` for ranks 2–3) is the "deciding owner kind is verified" input to category confidence. Trian, 3G, BDT and JAB read `pe_firm` (their own Form ADV), so whether their minority stakes in public companies (Wendy's, Burger King, Popeyes) change those companies' categories is this ticket's.
- **From chains "Research: Full category audit"** (2026-11-07): read literally, Form ADV `Any PE Funds = Y` also flags index and asset managers (BlackRock reports 125 PE funds; J.P. Morgan, FMR, State Street and T. Rowe Price also report Y), so a strict count would put nearly every public company in PE minority stake. The audit left them out, as the pilot did. Whether and how the engine excludes them (here, or by amending the PE-firm test from "Owner kind") is open for the developer.
- **From "Funds and their investment firm"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 amended; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4): `pe_firm` is renamed `private_equity`; new kinds `real_estate` and `venture_capital` with categories `real_estate_fund_owned` and `venture_capital_owned`, whose precedence is this ticket's. The stored (brand, PE owner) row is keyed to the firm when the fund has a `managed_by` edge, else to the fund. A listed parent stays a separate `public_company` above the firm, so a 13G filed by "BlackRock, Inc." under its CIK lands on a public company, not on a BlackRock adviser; that mostly settles the index-manager issue above, which this ticket still owns. This ticket is no longer blocked by "Funds and their investment firm".
- **From "Conflict, succession and co-investors"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 and ADR 0002 amended; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims` and `edges`): overlapping owners are co-owners unless their stated stakes can't all be true, so a brand can have several control chains (a family plus a conglomerate; a PE firm plus founders); the precedence among them is this ticket's. A brand's category is `sources_disagree` only when its contested edges lead to different categories; otherwise the category stands and only the owner line says sources disagree. For control and minority, a firm's stake is the sum of its funds' stated stakes, and the newest stake by as-of date decides (a 13G drop below 50% moves a brand from PE-owned to PE minority stake). This ticket is no longer blocked.
