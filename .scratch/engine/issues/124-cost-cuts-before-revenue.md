# Cost cuts before revenue

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Map: [engine](../map.md)

## Question

Which earlier decisions should change so the seeding pass costs less, and so nothing has to run or be paid for monthly until the site earns money, without hurting quality much?

- Largest seeding row: EDGAR full-text search, every hit read whole ("EDGAR full-text search and ownership filings" Q3 and Q4): 60,000 to 225,000 filings, $600 to $5,625. Brand-name hits are mostly lenders' schedules, landlords' tenant lists and mortgage-pool prospectuses, and EDGAR found no deal document for any of 13 private test deals ("Retrieval fan-out, tracked entities and refresh cadence" Q5 and its side finding).
- Decisions in reach: engine 70 Q3 (caps) and Q4 (whole reads); engine 30 (newsroom first crawls); engine 22 Q7 (a new version re-reads everything); engine 37 (cadences) and the open 119; chains 60 (search and the report form call FastAPI from the browser; Cloudflare builds from the public read API) and the open 40, 43 and 118.

## Grilling

### Round 1 (2026-11-20)

- Asked: Q1 filter EDGAR hits by filer before reading; Q2 whole reads only for the brand's company, its owners and deal-type filings, windows for other long filings; Q3 newsroom first-crawl filter; Q4 no automatic re-reads when the prompt or model changes, and seeding after p10; Q5 no scheduled refreshes before revenue, with an operator "new deals" pass; Q6 the engine on the developer's machine, a static search index and a free report function.
- **Q3 dropped** (developer, after the newsroom test): the first crawl keeps reading every release in full, as "Rosters, portfolio pages and the registry" already rules. The filter skipped little without losing ownership facts, and at the measured $0.00136 per release the full first crawl costs about $61 to $204.
- Q1 and Q2 wait on the third-party windows test (`.scratch/engine/research/edgar-third-party-windows.md`, running). Q4 to Q6 are unanswered.

Q7: skip 10-Qs filed by the brand's company and owners. Q8: send the SEC's requested headers (`Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate`) from the first request.

- **Measured for Q7, rate** (2026-11-21, `.scratch/engine/research/ten-q-deals-missing-from-ten-k.md`, code plus reading, no AI calls): 22 consumer companies, 734 10-Qs and 261 10-Ks from 2015 to 2026, about 420 real deals named in 10-Qs. - **Answered** (developer, 2026-11-21, "yes to all six"):

Amends "EDGAR full-text search and ownership filings" Q4.
  - **Q4:** a new prompt or model version reads new documents only; re-reading older documents is an operator choice and can target a slice (for example documents that produced facts); seeding waits until p10 is approved. Amends "Extraction execution" Q7.
  - **Q5:** no refresh runs on a schedule before revenue; refreshes are operator commands, including a cheap "new deals" pass (wire feeds, new newsroom releases, new deal-type EDGAR filings for tracked names). Changes the cadences of "Retrieval fan-out, tracked entities and refresh cadence"; direction for "Refresh schedule and next-check date".
  - **Q6:** before revenue the engine runs on the developer's machine (the `docker compose` stack); the static site is built there and uploaded to Cloudflare Pages; search is an index built into the site; the "Report a mistake" form is a small free Cloudflare function.   - **Q7 (narrowed):** full-text search reads every 10-Q filed by the brand's company or its owners after their latest 10-K and skips older ones; the 10-K counts with its exhibits. Amends "EDGAR full-text search and ownership filings" Q3.
  - **Q8:** every SEC request carries the headers the SEC's access guidelines ask for (a user agent and `Accept-Encoding: gzip, deflate`) from the first request, at most 1 request a second; a refusal stops SEC requests rather than being retried around. The user agent itself stays with "Operational identity, budgets and guardrail values".

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-21 in a grilling session (three rounds, recorded under Grilling), with four measurements: `research/edgar-quote-distance.md`, `research/edgar-third-party-windows.md`, `research/newsroom-filter-test.md` and `research/ten-q-deals-missing-from-ten-k.md` (DeepSeek spend $1.37; balance about $2.24 after).

- **EDGAR full-text search:** a hit is read whole only when the brand's own company or one of its tracked owners filed it; every other hit is read as windows of about 8,000 characters around each mention plus the cover page, no filer type skipped. 10-Qs from the brand's company or owners are read only when filed after the latest 10-K (the 10-K counts with its exhibits). SEC requests carry the SEC's requested headers from the first request, at most 1 a second, and a refusal stops them.
- **Newsrooms:** unchanged; every release is read on the first crawl (Q3 dropped after the test: no filter skipped much without losing facts, and the full crawl costs about $61 to $204 at $0.00136 a release).
- **Prompt and model versions:** a new version reads new documents only; re-reads are an operator choice and can target a slice; seeding waits until p10 is approved.
- **No timers before revenue:** every recurring job is a step of one operator command, the pass: a new Form ADV month if out, retries, the optional "new deals" pass, the 18-month staleness sweep, recompute and the drift check, the golden check, and the site build and upload. Nothing runs between passes; cards show no "Next check due" line.
- **Hosting before revenue:** the engine runs on the developer's machine; the static site is uploaded to Cloudflare Pages (free); search is an index built into the site; the "Report a mistake" form is a small free Cloudflare function. A paid host returns once the site earns money.
- **Rough effect on cash:** one seeding pass of DeepSeek reads from $854 to $7,735 down to roughly $400 to $3,700; a month at launch from $110 to $1,730 down to about $1 to $5 plus $2 to $25 per pass run; a prompt or model version from $850 to $7,700 down to $0 unless a re-read is chosen. Claude work stays inside the $100 Max plan.
- Docs: no glossary or ADR change (operational, reversible once revenue starts).

Amended: "EDGAR full-text search and ownership filings" (Q3, Q4, SEC headers), "Extraction execution" (Q7, golden check, unreadable sweep), "Retrieval fan-out, tracked entities and refresh cadence" (the pass), "Edge lifecycle" (staleness sweep), "Category storage and recompute" (drift check), "Form ADV and IRS exempt-org ingestion" (monthly load), chains "Website stack and hosting" (home machine, static search, report function, builds) and chains "Source-link submissions" (form mechanism).

Handed on: "Refresh schedule and next-check date" (cadences once timers return), "Hosting, storage and deployment" (the home setup, the function, backups, the later paid host), "Search and the demand log" (static index, demand log), "Operational identity, budgets and guardrail values" (per-pass caps, user-agent wording), "Task: Draft prompt p10 and run the approval check" (seed after p10), "Past coverage through DeepSeek Search" (results per query, the largest open seeding row), chains "Website pages and shareable cards".
