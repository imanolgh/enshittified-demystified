# Conflict, succession and co-investors

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 15  (Predicate semantics)
Map: [engine](../map.md)

## Question

When do two ownership claims conflict, when is a later claim a change of owner (succession), how are co-investors told apart from conflicts, and what happens with undated claims?

## Context

- ADR 0001:12: a hop is control unless a quote states <50% or "minority". ADR 0001:15: a new controlling-owner claim ends the prior hop. ADR 0002:10: conflict = different controlling owners over overlapping dates; co-investors are not conflicts. → Two co-investors with no stated stakes are both "control", hence a conflict, which ADR 0002 says they aren't.
- Most portfolio and roster claims have no date: rules for undated vs dated claims, event_date vs retrieved_at.
- **Several non-PE control chains** (family + conglomerate): conflict or precedence?
- **Conflicts on non-ownership predicates**: two filings with different `lends_to` principal or allocation % (ADR 0002 defines conflict only for controlling owners).
- **"sources disagree" — fact state or business category?** GLOSSARY:79–81 and CLAUDE.md:26 describe a fact state with links to each source; evidence_layer_plan.md:122 and ADR 0001:20 make it a business category; edge confidence has no disagree value. Storage options: conflicts table, edge status, or computed from claims at read time.
- **From "Predicate semantics"** (2026-11-05; role table in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): `acquired_by` and `owned_by` claims for the same pair are one ownership hop, published as `owned_by`; `acquired_by` gives the start date. `operated_by` is a separate hop kind, so a new operator of a location (a franchise resale) is succession on `operated_by`.
- **From "ExtractedClaim and the extraction result envelope"** (2026-11-06): claims carry `event_date_precision` (`year|month|day`); a year-only date is the first day of that year with precision `year`. How precision affects "overlapping dates" and which claim is later is this ticket's.
- **From "Claim value schemas"** (2026-11-06; schemas in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): `acquired_by` carries `deal_status` (`announced|completed`); only `completed` backs a hop, and a later `completed` claim for the same pair takes over from an announcement. A deal called off after announcement, two sources with the same as-of date that disagree, and stakes that change over time are this ticket's.
- **From "Funds and their investment firm"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 amended; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4): hops to a fund and to the firm that manages it, or to two funds of one firm, count as one owner: they never end each other as a succession and never count as sources disagreeing, and a sale to the same firm's continuation fund changes no category. A fund has one manager at a time: a newer `managed_by` claim naming a different firm ends the older edge (events row) by the values-over-time as-of-date rule. The mechanics of both are this ticket's.

## Comments

### Round 1 (2026-11-07)

Facts read (chains audit quotes): new investors usually join rather than replace ("THL is investing alongside existing financial sponsor Linden Capital Partners in an equal partnership", Smile Doctors; "General Atlantic joins existing investor SkyKnight Capital"; "Sycamore is acquiring the business in partnership with Stefano Pessina and his family", Walgreens), and real sales name the seller ("Warburg Pincus has acquired the company from affiliates of Wafra Inc.", El Car Wash; AEA and an ADIA subsidiary "acquired AmeriVet from Imperial Capital Group"; "Pugh acquired a majority stake in 200-unit Workout Anytime from founders"). Pending deal: Nestlé's sale of Nature's Bounty and Puritan's Pride to Yellow Wood Partners (agreed 2026-09-01). Checked for a split: not needed, because conflicts on loans and positions are already settled by "Claim value schemas" (each BDC holding is its own edge; the newest filing wins).

- **What ends an ownership hop** (Q1; ADR 0001 time rule amended): only (1) a document naming that owner as the seller, or the owner saying it sold or exited; (2) a completed acquisition stating a stake that can't be true beside the owner's stated stake (a 2024 stated majority ends a 2019 stated majority; a hop with no stated stake is never ended this way); (3) 18 months without confirmation (mechanics: "Edge lifecycle"). Otherwise owners whose hops overlap are co-owners (Smile Doctors: THL and Linden; Walgreens: Sycamore and the Pessina family; both PE-owned).
- **What counts as a conflict** (Q2; ADR 0002 amended): owners conflict only when their stated stakes can't all be true at once and neither ends the other (two different owners each with a stated majority in the same period, or stated stakes over 100%). Ownership loops stay a conflict. Co-owners with no stated stakes (a family plus a conglomerate, a PE firm plus founders) never conflict; which category wins is "Category precedence and storage".
- **Undated claims** (Q3): an undated claim takes its document's own date (dateline, filing date), else the fetch date (storage: "Document storage, fetch log and last-confirmed"). It can start or confirm a hop, and an undated exit ("Realized: El Car Wash") ends one as of that date. It never restarts a hop a dated claim ended: Wafra's page still listing El Car Wash in 2026, after the dated 2022 sale, is ignored as out of date, neither a conflict nor a restart. Only a dated claim after the end (a buyback) restarts a hop.
- **Precision** (Q4): dates compare at the coarser precision; in the same period, the claim from the later-dated document is later; if that ties too, neither is later, a seller or exit claim still ends the hop (naming a seller implies it owned first), and stated stakes that can't both be true are a conflict under Q2.
- **Deals called off** (Q5): `deal_status` gains `terminated` (the quote says the deal was terminated, called off or won't close); it ends the pending deal for that pair, with an events row. A pending deal with neither completion nor termination for 18 months stops showing as pending (mechanics: "Edge lifecycle"; display: the card ticket). Example from memory: Kroger's deal for Albertsons, called off in December 2024.
- **One-at-a-time relationships** (Q6): `operated_by` and `managed_by` hold one at a time, so a newer claim naming a different company ends the older edge without naming a seller (a franchise resale). Two different operators or managers with the same date are a conflict on that edge. `lends_to` and `holds_position` never conflict (each BDC holding is its own edge; newest filing wins).

### Round 2 (2026-11-07)

- **Recording a seller or exit** (Q7, option a): `owned_by` gains `holding` (`current|former`, default `current`). "Warburg Pincus has acquired the company from affiliates of Wafra Inc." gives Warburg's `acquired_by` and an `owned_by` for Wafra with `holding: former`, dated like the deal; the same claim covers a seller's own sale release and "Realized" or "Exited" listings. A `former` claim ends that owner's hop as of its date; with no earlier claim for the pair it records a past hop with an unknown start (feeds "formerly PE-owned"). When the same document says the seller keeps a stake ("Eagle Merchant Partners will retain a minority interest"; "Certain members of the Rizzuto family have retained a minority ownership stake in Conair"), the `current` claim with that stake wins within the document, so the hop continues as a minority from the deal date. Turned down: a `seller_names` list on `acquired_by` (misses exits and listings) and a new predicate.
- **Storing and showing a conflict** (Q8): edge confidence gains `sources_disagree`; a contested edge is never shown as fact, and the card lists each side with links (ownership, managers, operators, stakes). A brand's category is `sources_disagree` only when the contested edges lead to different categories; if every reading gives the same category it stands, and only the owner line says sources disagree (two PE firms each with a stated majority in the same year: PE-owned stands). Loops stay `sources_disagree`. A fund with disputed managers inherits a kind only if every disputed manager has the same kind. Turned down: a conflicts table, and recomputing from claims on every read. Whether the category is stored: "Category precedence and storage".
- **Which stake counts** (Q9): the newest stake by as-of date decides control or minority (a later 13G at 45% would move Jersey Mike's from PE-owned to PE minority stake, with an events row); a stated 0% is an exit (a `former` claim). At the same as-of date an official filing beats a non-official source, else the later-filed document wins ("Claim value schemas"), except that two non-official stakes on opposite sides of 50% dispute the hop's control (Q8). Amends the "Claim value schemas" tie rule for ownership stakes only.
- **One owner across a fund and its firm** (Q10): naming the firm as seller ends hops to the firm and all its funds; naming a fund ends only that fund's hop, so a sale from The Resolute Fund V to a Jordan continuation fund keeps the brand PE-owned with no "formerly" history and the row keeps the firm's first start date. The owner's stake is the sum of its funds' stated stakes (Blackstone Capital Partners IX plus Blackstone Private Equity Strategies Fund at Jersey Mike's) for control, for Q1's second ending and for Q2's conflicts; a firm's funds never conflict with or end each other.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-07 in a grilling session (two rounds, recorded under Comments).

- **Endings** (ADR 0001 time rule amended): an ownership hop ends only when (1) a document names that owner as the seller, or the owner says it sold or exited (an `owned_by` claim with `holding: former`); (2) a completed acquisition states a stake that can't be true beside the owner's stated stake; or (3) it goes 18 months without confirmation. Any other overlap is **co-ownership** (THL and Linden at Smile Doctors; Sycamore and the Pessina family at Walgreens).
- **Conflicts** (ADR 0002 amended): only stated stakes that can't all be true at once, with neither ending the other (two owners each with a stated majority in the same period, or stakes over 100%), plus loops. For `operated_by` and `managed_by`, which hold one at a time, a newer claim naming a different company ends the older edge, and two companies with the same date conflict. Loans and positions never conflict.
- **Storage:** edge confidence gains `sources_disagree`; a contested edge is never shown as fact. A brand's category is `sources_disagree` only when the contested readings lead to different categories; otherwise the category stands and the owner line says sources disagree.
- **Dates:** an undated claim takes its document's own date, else the fetch date; it can start, confirm or (as an exit) end a hop but never restarts one a dated claim ended (an out-of-date portfolio listing is ignored). Dates compare at the coarser precision; ties go to the later-dated document; if that ties, a seller or exit claim still ends the hop and incompatible stakes conflict.
- **Deals:** `deal_status` gains `terminated`, which ends a pending deal; a pending deal with no completion or termination for 18 months stops showing as pending.
- **Stakes:** the newest stake by as-of date decides control or minority; a stated 0% is an exit. At the same as-of date an official filing wins, else the later-filed document, except that two non-official stakes on opposite sides of 50% dispute the hop's control ("Claim value schemas" tie rule amended for ownership stakes).
- **One owner across a fund and its firm:** naming the firm as seller ends hops to the firm and all its funds; naming a fund ends only that fund's; a continuation-fund sale changes no category. The owner's stake is the sum of its funds' stated stakes.
- **Seller keeps a stake:** within one document a `current` claim beats a `former` claim for the same pair. Because the claim hash ignores value fields, a quote that both names the seller and says it keeps a stake gives only the `current` claim.
- Docs: ADR 0001 and ADR 0002 amended; glossary "Sources disagree", new "Co-owner" and "Pending deal"; evidence_layer_plan.md §4 and Stage 6; CLAUDE.md conflict invariant; conformance-tests skill. No new ADR.
