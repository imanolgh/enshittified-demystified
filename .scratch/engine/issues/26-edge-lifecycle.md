# Edge lifecycle: ending, retraction and staleness

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 20, 25  (Document storage, fetch log and last-confirmed; Conflict, succession and co-investors)
Map: [engine](../map.md)

## Question

How do edges start, end, get retracted, go stale and get re-confirmed — and how do we stop re-derivation from resurrecting a retracted edge?

## Context

- **Ended vs retracted**: one column `retracted_at` (evidence_layer_plan.md:114–119); "end-dated" used both for wrong facts and true-but-ended facts (ADR 0001:15; research_flows.md:162). Needs start date ("formerly PE-owned {years}"), end date, last_confirmed_at, status; current value for PC edges.
- **Mutation policy**: on confidence change (reported→verified, research_flows.md:101) update in place + event, or new row? stack_fastapi.md:44 says "edge upsert". Unique per (from, to, relation) or per ownership period (sold then re-acquired)?
- **Retraction**: build_roadmap.md:157 = "end-date edge, claims untouched" — but edges are derived, so accepted claims re-create it; evidence_layer_plan.md:111 has claim status `retracted`; :134 "retract and add a new one". A manual `POST /internal/edges/{id}/retract` contradicts ADR 0002:17 (no hand-editing facts). Who triggers a retraction, which automated cases exist, what is the reason vocabulary? (A takedown/dispute intake is out of scope but the likeliest real trigger.)
- **Disappearance**: departed loans (research_flows.md:162), removed roster locations (:103), plans missing from a 5500 reload (:164). Absence can't be quoted — what records the end, after how many consecutive misses?
- **Staleness**: what makes a `reported` edge stale; "hunt for 2nd source" undefined (research_flows.md:113,166; build_roadmap.md:129). The 18-month last-confirmed sweep (ADR 0001:15) is in no Beat table.
- **Append-only vs "claim updates"** (CLAUDE.md:25 vs research_flows.md:162).
- Concurrency (advisory lock per business or a single-concurrency publish queue) and incremental re-derivation (recompute touched (s,p,o) groups, emit events only on change) — defaultable.
- **From "Claim producers and the claims door"** (2026-11-05; summary in evidence_layer_plan.md "Producers and the door"): claims carry `producer_version`. Withdrawing every claim from a buggy producer version (retraction plus events) is this ticket's.
- **From "Predicate semantics"** (2026-11-05; role table in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): edges: from = the claim's subject; `relation` is never `acquired_by` (it backs an `owned_by` edge). `location_brands` rows are identity data, not edges.
- **From "Entity lifecycle"** (2026-11-06; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `entities`): on a merge, the duplicate's edges are retracted and the survivor's recomputed from both entities' claims (events row); an unmerge reverses it. Withdrawing a whole `producer` + `producer_version` retracts its identity rows (`retracted_at`); how its claims and edges are withdrawn is this ticket's. In the store-map follow-up, a store that drops out of its locator gets `status = closed`; that diff is this ticket's and "Rosters…"'s.
- **From "Claim identity"** (2026-11-06; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): a claim with a null entity id never counts toward an edge. Code may re-resolve a claim to a different entity when resolution rules improve; that recomputes the affected edges, with an events row.
- **From "Claim value schemas"** (2026-11-06; schemas in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): an edge's value comes from its newest accepted claim by as-of date (`event_date`, else the document's date), and a later-filed document wins a tie (a 10-Q/A). A loan missing from the next Schedule, meaning it ended, is this ticket's.
- **From "Funds and their investment firm"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 amended; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4): the role-table check runs when edges are built, not when a claim is accepted. An accepted claim's edge forms only once the current entity types fit, so when code types a company as a fund (a Form ADV record), the edges of its earlier accepted claims form then, with no claim status change. How that recompute and its events row work is this ticket's.
- **From "Conflict, succession and co-investors"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 and ADR 0002 amended; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims` and `edges`): an ownership hop ends only by (1) an `owned_by` claim with `holding: former` for that pair (a named seller, a sale release, a "Realized" listing; naming a firm ends hops to the firm and all its funds), (2) a completed acquisition stating a stake that can't be true beside the owner's stated stake, or (3) 18 months without confirmation; every other overlap is co-ownership. A `former` claim with no earlier claim records a past hop with an unknown start. An undated claim never restarts a hop a dated claim ended; a dated claim after the end (a buyback) does. `operated_by` and `managed_by` hold one at a time: a newer claim naming a different company ends the older edge. `deal_status = terminated` ends a pending deal, and a pending deal with no completion or termination for 18 months stops showing as pending. Edge confidence gains `sources_disagree` for contested edges. How endings, restarts, the 18-month clocks and the recompute of disputed edges run is this ticket's. This ticket now waits only on "Document storage, fetch log and last-confirmed".
- **From "What 'checked' means and coverage states"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 amended; tables `document_reads` and `source_runs` in evidence_layer_plan.md §4): withdrawing a producer version also sets `retracted_at` on its `document_reads` rows, and every source run that relied on them stops counting; affected brands fall back to their previous complete check or "not yet checked" until the re-run finishes. The mechanics are this ticket's.
- **From "Document storage, fetch log and last-confirmed"** (2026-11-07; notes in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `documents` and `fetches`): a hop's last-confirmed date is the newest `last_seen_at` among the documents its accepted claims cite; a same-text re-fetch or a `304 Not Modified` advances it; a `404` or `410` is logged as `not_found` and confirms nothing. Whether repeated misses end a fact, and how the 18-month sweep reads `last_seen_at`, are this ticket's. The `fetches` log keeps attempts 90 days.

## Comments

### Round 1 (2026-11-07)

- **Split of mechanics** (Q0): incremental recompute (only affected edges, events only on visible change) and re-deriving edges after a type change, re-resolution or merge moved to "Category storage and recompute"; the lock-per-brand or single-publish-queue question moved to "Celery topology". This ticket keeps what happens to an edge.
- **Edge rows** (Q1): one row per ownership period (sold in 2019, bought back in 2024: two rows); a row changes in place with an events row per visible change (confidence, phase, end, value); a new row only for a new period. New columns: `phase`, `started_on` and `ended_on` (each with a precision), `end_reason`, `ended_by_document_id`, `last_confirmed_on`, `value_claim_id` (the claim whose value the edge shows, a foreign key, never a copy); `retracted_at` stays for edges that should never have existed.
- **Phases and endings** (Q2): `pending|active|unconfirmed|ended`; one row runs from announcement to end (Nestlé's agreed sale of Nature's Bounty to Yellow Wood is `pending`, and turns `active` on completion with `started_on` = the closing date). `end_reason`: `seller_or_exit`, `superseded_by_stake`, `replaced`, `absent_from_list`, `terminated`, `pending_expired` (18 months without completion). Only real endings feed "formerly PE-owned {years}"; `unconfirmed` is not an end. The developer asked whether fetches would run more often near a pending deal's closing (see Q7).
- **Retraction** (Q3): an edge is never retracted directly; code retracts claims or identity rows, and an edge left with no accepted, correctly typed claims is retracted (`retracted_at`, events row), so re-derivation can't resurrect it. Automated triggers only, no hand endpoint (ADR 0002): `producer_withdrawn`, `entity_merged`, `entity_unmerged`, `claim_re_resolved`, `entity_type_changed`. A takedown or dispute intake stays out of scope.
- **Withdrawing a producer version** (Q4): one operator command (1) sets that version's claims to `retracted`, (2) sets `retracted_at` on its identity rows (undoing merges they triggered), (3) retracts its document reads (source runs relying on them stop counting), (4) recomputes affected edges (no remaining support → retracted; other support → confidence and value recomputed), (5) writes one events row for the command with counts plus one per edge or category that visibly changed, none per claim, (6) queues a re-run with the fixed version.
- **Disappearance** (Q5): a `lends_to` or `holds_position` edge ends when the fund's next Schedule parses cleanly and lacks the holding (`absent_from_list`, `ended_on` = that Schedule's period end, `ended_by_document_id` = that filing); one miss is enough; an unparseable Schedule proves nothing (Express Wash Acquisition Company at BlackRock Direct Lending, 2026-06-30). Ownership never ends by absence. Stores and roster locations (store-map follow-up, local businesses) close after missing from two complete crawls in a row. A `404` or `410` never ends anything alone.
- **Staleness** (Q6): a daily Beat sweep sets `unconfirmed` (events row) on any edge whose `last_confirmed_on` is over 18 months old; the row shows "last confirmed {date}", stops counting toward categories, and returns to `active` when a later document confirms it (Ideal Dental's 2021 Blackstone Tactical Opportunities hop would read "last confirmed 2021"). The "hunt for a second source" is dropped; a second source comes from the brand's normal re-checks.
- **Expected closing** (Q7, revised after the developer asked to track a deal's date and why "in the coming weeks" wouldn't count): `acquired_by` gains `expected_close_text` on `announced` claims (the phrase as written, in the quote). Code maps it to `expected_close_from` and `expected_close_to` on the pending edge from a table in code, with relative phrases anchored to the document's date (`published_on`, else first fetch): a quarter or half → that period; a month → that month; "by {date}" or "by year-end" → document date to that date; "in the coming days" → +1 month; "in the coming weeks" → +3 months (the developer's number); "shortly", "soon", "promptly" → +3 months; "in the coming months" → +6 months; "later this year" → to December 31 of the document's year; "early/mid/late {year}" → January–April, May–August, September–December. Unknown phrases go to the operator report and are mapped by a code change (stored phrases re-mapped, no new LLM calls); no stated timing → no range. A newer announced claim moves the range. The developer's cadence rule goes to "Retrieval fan-out, tracked entities and refresh cadence": a brand's own sources run faster while its pending deal's range approaches and runs, return to normal after it passes or the deal completes or is called off, and speed up again if a delay moves the range.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-07 in a grilling session (one round, recorded under Comments). Recompute mechanics moved to "Category storage and recompute" and concurrency to "Celery topology".

- **Rows:** one edge row per ownership period, changed in place with an events row per visible change; a new row only for a new period (at most one open period per pair and relation). New columns `phase`, `started_on`/`ended_on` (with precision), `end_reason`, `ended_by_document_id`, `last_confirmed_on`, `value_claim_id`, `expected_close_from`/`expected_close_to`.
- **Phases:** `pending` (announced; never counts), `active`, `unconfirmed` (18 months unconfirmed: shows "last confirmed {date}", stops counting, returns to `active` when confirmed) and `ended`. End reasons: `seller_or_exit`, `superseded_by_stake`, `replaced`, `absent_from_list`, `terminated`, `pending_expired`. Only real endings feed "formerly PE-owned".
- **Retraction** follows claims: code retracts claims or identity rows, and an edge with no remaining accepted, correctly typed claims is retracted, so re-derivation can't resurrect it. Triggers: `producer_withdrawn`, `entity_merged`, `entity_unmerged`, `claim_re_resolved`, `entity_type_changed`; no hand endpoint.
- **Withdrawal command:** retract the version's claims, identity rows and document reads; recompute affected edges; one events row for the command plus one per visible change; queue a re-run.
- **Disappearance:** a clean Schedule missing a holding ends its `lends_to`/`holds_position` edge at that period end (one miss); ownership never ends by absence; stores and roster locations close after two complete misses; a `404` never ends anything alone.
- **Staleness:** a daily Beat sweep; no "hunt for a second source".
- **Expected closing:** `acquired_by` gains `expected_close_text`; code maps it to a range on the pending edge from a phrase table (relative phrases anchored to the document's date; "in the coming weeks" = 3 months); unknown phrases go to the operator report; a newer claim moves the range. The developer's rule for faster fetches near a deal goes to "Retrieval fan-out, tracked entities and refresh cadence".
