# Funds and their investment firm

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 14  (Owner kind: storage, evidence and the PE-firm test)
Map: [engine](../map.md)

## Question

How is a fund linked to the investment firm that runs it without adding an ownership hop, does a PE ownership chain end at the fund or at the firm (and so which one the separation degree counts to), and does a fund take its firm's owner kind?

## Context

- Split from "Owner kind: storage, evidence and the PE-firm test" on 2026-11-06 (developer ruling, to keep each ticket to one session).
- **Fund ↔ firm**: the chain walk stops at "pe_firm (fund or firm)" (ADR 0001:11) and the card "names the firm without adding a hop" (:13). Needs a relation outside the predicate set (`owned_by` would add a hop) and a rule on whether a fund inherits its firm's kind ("pe_firm" as a fund's kind is a misnomer).
- **Does a PE chain end at a fund or a firm?** GLOSSARY:7–8,15–16,19–20 count hops "to a private equity fund"; ADR 0001:11,13 keeps one row per (business, PE firm); research_flows.md:71 counts degree 2 to a firm.
- **From "Predicate semantics"** (2026-11-05; role table in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `claims`): a fund is never the subject of `owned_by`, so the fund–firm link needs its own form here (an LP stake isn't a hop either). Owner-kind evidence is not a predicate. A brand's owner may now be a fund, firm or family directly (ADR 0005 amended).
- **From "Entity lifecycle"** (2026-11-06): code may change an entity's type from `company` to `fund` or `investment_firm` when a Form ADV identity record matches it, with an events row.
- **Facts** from "Research: Form ADV and IRS exempt-org bulk data": fund-level rows (fund name, `805-` private fund id, `Fund Type` = "Private Equity Fund", and the fund-to-adviser link) are only in the historical archive through 2024; funds reported after 2025 appear only in per-adviser IAPD PDFs. Quotes often name the firm, not the fund ("Roark Capital acquired Subway").
- A brand's owner may be a fund, firm or family named directly (ADR 0005 amended by "Predicate semantics").
- **From "Owner kind: storage, evidence and the PE-firm test"** (2026-11-07; ADR 0001 amended; table in evidence_layer_plan.md §4 `owner_kind_evidence`): owner kind lives in `owner_kind_evidence` rows and is computed onto `entities.owner_kind`. Form ADV `Any PE Funds = Y` sits on the adviser (the firm); whether a fund takes its firm's kind, and what a fund described as "a private equity fund" means, are this ticket's. A BDC is `other_investment_fund` from its `814-` number.

## Comments

### Round 1 (2026-11-07)

Facts read: the audit's quotes name the firm far more often than the fund, but some name the fund: Kirkland's release says The Jordan Company bought Dental365 "through an affiliate of The Resolute Fund V, L.P."; Cortec's own site names "Cortec Group Fund VI, L.P." for Lap of Love; Lifetime Brands' proxy names only "Centre Partners V, L.P." (26.2%); Jersey Mike's prospectus names "Blackstone Capital Partners IX L.P. and Blackstone Private Equity Strategies Fund L.P.". In 13 audited categories (about 374 brand rows) one real estate fund appeared (Sculptor Real Estate's 2023 growth investment in Wildcat's car wash platform, stake not stated); venture capital appeared more often (Institutional Venture Partners 17.6% of Lulus; VC-only advisers behind Small Door and Petfolk).

- **Fund to firm link** (Q1, option a): a new predicate `managed_by` (fund → investment_firm), a claim like any other, never an ownership hop, no value fields. Any producer may make one: the Form ADV ETL (each archive fund row names its adviser; official filing, so it self-verifies), the LLM reading a release ("an affiliate of Cortec Group"), or a BDC's 10-K ("externally managed by"). Turned down: an identity table written only by code producers (loses links stated in releases, and 2025+ funds), and folding fund names into the firm as aliases (loses fund-level kinds, BDCs and the parked retirement side's `allocates_to`).
- **Where a PE chain ends** (Q2): the walk stops at the first owner whose kind is PE, whether the hop names a fund or a firm; `managed_by` never adds a hop. A fund and the firm that manages it count as one PE owner: the stored (brand, PE owner) row is keyed to the firm when the fund has a `managed_by` edge, else to the fund. Degree counts hops to whichever PE entity comes first (Dental365 is degree 1 whether a quote names The Resolute Fund V or The Jordan Company; Jersey Mike's two Blackstone funds give one Blackstone row; Bright Smiles → Smile Partners → Fund X stays degree 2). Glossary Separation degree and PE-owned reworded to match.
- **A fund's kind** (Q3): a fund's own evidence comes first. The Form ADV archive's per-fund `Fund Type` is rank 1 for that fund; a BDC keeps its `814-` rule; a fund's own description goes through the term table. A fund with no kind evidence takes its manager's kind through the `managed_by` edge, verified only if the edge and the firm's kind are both verified, else reported. Kind flows down only; a firm never takes a kind from its funds. Caveat recorded: each adviser picks its funds' types, so some real estate funds may be filed as "Private Equity Fund" (not checked for Blackstone).
- **Kinds for real estate and venture capital funds** (Q3a, option c; raised by the developer): new owner kinds `real_estate` and `venture_capital`, with categories `real_estate_fund_owned` and `venture_capital_owned`. For a fund: its Form ADV fund type ("Real Estate Fund" → `real_estate`, "Venture Capital Fund" → `venture_capital`, "Private Equity Fund" → private equity; hedge, securitized asset and liquidity funds → `other_investment_fund`; "Other Private Fund" gives nothing). For a firm: the monthly file's counts by fund type; any PE fund still makes it private equity, a firm whose funds are all real estate or all VC takes that kind, any other mix takes `other_investment_fund`. Hedge, credit, securitized-asset and liquidity funds stay in `other_investment_fund`. Precedence to "Category precedence and storage"; display wording ("VC-backed") to the card ticket. Limit: per-fund types are in bulk only through 2024, so a newer fund or a quote naming only the firm falls back to the firm's kind.
- **Rename** (Q4): the owner kind `pe_firm` becomes `private_equity` everywhere it's live (ADR 0001, glossary, evidence_layer_plan.md, research_flows.md, open tickets); resolved tickets keep the old word as history. Categories unchanged.
- **Housing** (Q5, option c; raised by the developer, who would like users to see which apartments and houses for rent or sale are owned or run by PE): a separate parked effort, not chains and brands v1 and not local businesses. Facts given: building ownership is in about 3,100 county assessor and recorder systems; national compilations (Regrid, ATTOM, CoreLogic) are paid secondary sources the project rule excludes; deeds name LLCs and Delaware LLCs don't disclose members; some cities publish rental registries (New York City's HPD registrations name owner and managing agent); individuals own most homes, so a firm privacy rule is needed; listings come from MLSs licensed to brokers and from sites whose terms forbid scraping, so the workable shape is a user entering an address or landlord name; a building's owner would need a location → `owned_by` role, a later schema change. Records: a parked housing map with a destination sketch and one research ticket (public sources tying a building to its owner, and the privacy rule), not run now; ADR 0004 amended to list it as parked; "landlord brands" (single-family rental companies that own the homes they rent under their own name, such as Invitation Homes, Progress Residential, FirstKey Homes; owners from memory, to be confirmed) added as a candidate for chains "Product categories", which decides whether it's in v1 and whether property managers such as Greystar are included.

### Round 2 (2026-11-07)

Facts read: the audit's Form ADV table shows firms filing under several registrations (KKR: Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. Y 553 and KKR Credit Advisors (US) LLC Y 38; L Catterton: Catterton Management Company N, L Catterton Europe SAS Y 6, L Catterton Asia Advisors Y 3; BlackRock: BlackRock Capital Investment Advisors Y 125, BlackRock Financial Management Y 73) and a joint-venture adviser (FS/KKR Advisor, N, adviser to FS KKR Capital Corp).

- **One firm, several registrations** (Q6, option a): one `investment_firm` entity per firm as the public names it, holding each adviser registration's CRD as an identifier; `Any PE Funds = Y` on any registration counts for the firm (L Catterton becomes private equity at rank 1). A joint-venture adviser owned by two firms (FS/KKR Advisor) stays its own firm, owned by both. Code groups registrations from Form ADV Schedule D Section 7.A (related advisers); whether the bulk files carry it, and how current, goes to "Form ADV and IRS exempt-org ingestion".
- **Listed parent** (Q7): a public company above a firm (Blackstone Inc., KKR & Co. Inc., Ares Management Corp; The Goldman Sachs Group above Goldman Sachs Asset Management; BlackRock, Inc.) stays a separate company with its own ticker and `public_company` kind, linked to the firm by an ordinary `owned_by` hop from an SEC filing. The walk stops at the fund or firm before reaching it, and kinds never flow up: MyEyeDr. reads PE-owned through West Street Capital Partners VII and Goldman Sachs never becomes a PE firm; a 13G filed by "BlackRock, Inc." under its CIK lands on the parent, which mostly settles the index-manager issue on "Category precedence and storage" (that ticket still owns it). Which entity a bare "Blackstone" means goes to "Entity resolution algorithm"; "part of Blackstone Inc. (NYSE: BX)" on the card to the card ticket. Turned down: merging the parent into the firm (needs a rule that an investment firm's ticker doesn't count, and no filing field reliably tells Blackstone Inc. from The Goldman Sachs Group).
- **Quotes naming both, and one owner** (Q8): the LLM returns an ownership claim for each owner the quote names as buyer or holder, and a `managed_by` claim when the quote says the fund is the firm's ("managed by", "advised by", "its affiliate, Cortec Group Fund VI, L.P."). Code treats hops to a fund and to its own firm, or to two funds of the same firm, as one owner: they never end each other as a succession, never count as sources disagreeing, and a sale to the same firm's continuation fund changes no category. Prompt wording to "Extraction execution"; the rule to "Conflict, succession and co-investors".
- **A fund named before Form ADV types it** (Q9): (1) for each firm in the graph, the Form ADV connector reads the firm's current IAPD Form ADV PDF for its fund list (names and `805-` numbers as text; type checkboxes not readable), creating identity records that type the funds and `managed_by` claims that link them; cadence to "Form ADV and IRS exempt-org ingestion". (2) The role-table check moves from claim acceptance to edge building: a claim whose quote passes is accepted, and its edge forms only once the current entity types fit, as a null id never counts today; when code later types a company as a fund, the edge forms with no status change. Amends "Predicate semantics" (a type mismatch no longer rejects with `sanity_check_failed`). Turned down: letting an accepted `managed_by` claim retype its sides ("the hotel is managed by Highgate" would turn a hotel company into a fund).
- **One manager at a time** (Q10): a newer `managed_by` claim naming a different firm ends the older edge (events row), by the values-over-time as-of-date rule (Oaktree replacing Fifth Street's adviser at Fifth Street Finance in 2017, from memory); mechanics go with ownership succession in "Conflict, succession and co-investors".

## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-07 in a grilling session (two rounds, recorded under Comments).

- **Link:** new predicate `managed_by` (fund → investment_firm): a claim from any producer (Form ADV ETL, LLM, BDC 10-K), never an ownership hop, no value fields. A fund has one manager at a time; a newer claim naming a different firm ends the older edge.
- **Chain end:** the walk stops at the first private-equity owner, fund or firm. A fund and the firm that manages it, or two funds of one firm, count as one PE owner: the (brand, PE owner) row is keyed to the firm, the degree counts to whichever comes first, and hops to them never end each other or count as disagreeing.
- **A fund's kind:** its own evidence first (the Form ADV archive's per-fund `Fund Type` is rank 1; a BDC keeps its `814-` rule); otherwise its manager's kind, computed by code with no evidence row, verified only if the `managed_by` edge and the firm's kind are both verified. Kind flows down only.
- **Kinds** (ADR 0001 amended): `pe_firm` renamed `private_equity`; new kinds `real_estate` and `venture_capital` with categories `real_estate_fund_owned` and `venture_capital_owned`. A firm with any PE fund is `private_equity`; all real estate or all VC funds take that kind; any other mix is `other_investment_fund`. Starting terms move: "venture capital firm/fund" → `venture_capital`; "real estate investment firm", "real estate fund" → `real_estate`.
- **Firms:** one `investment_firm` entity per firm as the public names it, holding each adviser registration's CRD; `Any PE Funds = Y` on any registration counts (L Catterton becomes rank 1). Joint-venture advisers stay their own firm. A listed parent (Blackstone Inc., The Goldman Sachs Group, BlackRock, Inc.) is a separate `public_company` above the firm by an ordinary hop that the walk never reaches, so a 13G filed by BlackRock, Inc. lands on a public company.
- **Typing:** the Form ADV connector reads each in-graph firm's current IAPD Form ADV PDF for its fund list (names and `805-` numbers), typing funds by identity records. The role-table check moves from claim acceptance to edge building, so a claim naming a not-yet-typed fund forms its edge once code types it ("Predicate semantics" amended).
- **Housing** (raised by the developer): a separate parked effort, [housing map](../../housing/map.md), with one research ticket; ADR 0004 amended. "Landlord brands" is a candidate for chains "Product categories".
- Docs: ADR 0001 and ADR 0004 amended; glossary Separation degree, PE-owned, Owner kind, Investment firm and Fund; evidence_layer_plan.md §4; CLAUDE.md predicate invariant; research_flows.md. No new ADR.

Handed on:
