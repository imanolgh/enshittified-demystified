## Answer

Resolved 2026-11-15 in one grilling session (four rounds, Q1 to Q17; rulings under Comments). No test spending in this ticket; the approval check runs in the new task "Draft prompt p9 and run the approval check".

**Prompt rules**
- **Base (Q1):** the v8 test prompt plus the rules below; v5 stays a reference. Drafting fixes: no document-label sentence, nothing that assumes a named brand, repeated link text copied exactly (TSG's "[link: Close]").
- **Brand owner (Q2; GLOSSARY Brand):** a brand's owner is whoever owns its name, never a licensee. A trademark notice gives `owned_by` (brand to trademark owner, current). "Licensed from Y" or "used under license" gives `owned_by` to the licensor and nothing to the licensee. A brand list that says some brands are licensed without saying which gives no claim for unmarked brands. A line naming no brand gives nothing. An IP holding company is claimed as written (Q7: no code rule; in the test set 3 of about 25 notice-bearing documents named a subsidiary with no link upward, and such a brand reads "owner type not confirmed" until one is found, as for any subsidiary named alone).
- **Not the page owner's holdings (Q3):** the list-every-company rule covers only the page owner's own holdings. Sections the page says are something else ("Prior to <firm>", prior firm investments, team experience, deals advised on, loans or financings) get no claim, not even former. Team bios' deals at other firms get no claim. An adviser's deal stated in words counts as normal.
- **Not ownership (Q4):** "privately owned", "privately held", "independent", "run by family" and "family-operated" (only family-owned counts); "X, an affiliate of Y" on its own ("affiliate" counts only inside an ownership statement such as "backed by an affiliate of Point72" or a deal document's "Parent is an affiliate of Bansk Group"); stores or practices "independently owned and operated" or franchised; lending, financing, credit facilities and advisory work; copyright lines.
- **Groups and closures (Q5):** a quote naming a group's members gives a claim per member and none for an unnamed group ("a consortium"); the group phrase stays a described owner only when no members are named. A group's stake is never copied to a member (five sponsors each marked majority would make Chewy "sources disagree"). A business the document says was shut down gives `owned_by` holding former, dated at the closing (Mud Room, 2020).
- **Known limit (Q8):** a licensee's brand list that never mentions licensing (Meyer listing KitchenAid, Rachael Ray, Ayesha Curry, Farberware) still gives the licensee as owner. The product side is handed to chains-and-brands "Product categories".

**Approval check (Q6, Q9, Q10)**
- Two full runs on the 157 clean-text documents, no brand named, DeepSeek's own API off-peak, plus about 10 new documents fetched under the bot rules that test the new rules (a "used under license" page, a lender's portfolio page, another team page listing earlier firms' deals, a franchise "independently owned" page, an IP-company trademark notice).
- Bars per run: at least 138 of 218 known facts; at least 98% exact quotes, leaving out TSG's page; at most 1 lost answer in 314; no claim breaking a Q2 to Q5 rule in the targeted documents (counted by script); at least 100 Leonard Green claims on its page.
- Blind precision on 40 claims per run, judged by a separate agent that never sees the version: at least 37 fully correct, at most 1 wrong, none of the wrong ones on a new rule.
- A missed bar means fix, rerun the failing documents, then one more full run. No fallback-route check (Q10).
- Approval is the developer's commit of the passing prompt file, `.scratch/engine/research/extraction-prompt-p9.md` until the build, then `app/core/` unchanged. Production version numbers continue from the test numbers (first production prompt p9).
- Every later prompt or model version passes the same check before the developer commits it, with bars set against the last approved version: known facts down at most 3 per run, quotes at least 98%, blind precision at least 37 of 40 with at most 1 wrong. Each extraction error found in production adds its document to the targeted set.
- Cost about $2 to $2.50; the developer tops up DeepSeek by $5 (balance $1.18 on 2026-11-15).

**Fallback triggers (Q11 to Q17; corrects "Extraction execution" Q14)**
- **Down (Q11):** no answer (a connection failure, the 15-minute timeout, DeepSeek's own close after 10 minutes without starting) or HTTP 500, 502, 503 or 504.
- **Reachable but refusing (Q12):** never switches. A 402 (balance empty) or 401 (key rejected) pauses every DeepSeek read, records it and alerts; a free balance check every 5 minutes resumes them. A 429 slows down and retries on DeepSeek. Bad answers and request errors (400, 422) stay as ruled: `unreadable`, an alert, the 7-day sweep.
- **Outage (Q13, Q15):** three "down" attempts on one document open an incident. While it is open, single new documents from routine crawls go straight to the fallback; bulk work (the seeding pass, version re-reads, the daily sweep) waits for DeepSeek or the developer's command. A tiny DeepSeek test request every 5 minutes closes the incident. When OpenRouter answers 402 or 403 for credits or the key limit, or is down, reads wait; a free key-balance check every 5 minutes resumes them after the developer's top-up. An outage never makes a read `unreadable`. Alerts when an incident opens, when the fallback runs out of funds, and daily while one is open. About $0.009 per document through the fallback; the key limit is the spending cap.
- **Record (Q14):** new table `api_incidents` (provider, reason `down|balance_empty|key_rejected`, opened_at, closed_at, fallback_state `in_use|out_of_funds|down`, last_status, last_error), one row per incident; an empty DeepSeek balance gets a row too.
- **Operator commands (Q16, Q17):** status; check DeepSeek now; use the fallback (shows the waiting reads, estimated cost and the key's remaining limit, asks to confirm; DeepSeek coming back takes the rest back); back to DeepSeek. A manual resume keeps the off-peak hold for bulk work unless run with "now" (peak price).

Docs: GLOSSARY Brand (owner is whoever owns the name); evidence_layer_plan.md Stage 3 (prompt rules pointer; step 3 rewritten for the fallback triggers) and §4 (`extraction_requests.failure_reason` values now `connection|server_error|timeout|rate_limited|balance_empty|key_rejected|bad_request|output_limit|empty|refusal|bad_json`, matching Q11 and Q12; new `api_incidents`); CLAUDE.md stack line (fallback only when DeepSeek's API is down); correction note on "Extraction execution".
